Questions shop owners ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no card field on that form because there's nothing to pay. If we introduce paid placement later it will be for premium position only, and listings created now stay free.

What's in it for RepairRange?

A directory with real shops in it is worth more to customers than an empty one, and customers are what make the site useful. That's the whole trade — we need the coverage more than we need your $29.

Do I have to link back to you?

No. We'll offer you a badge for your own site because some shops like showing they're listed, but your listing doesn't depend on it and never will.

Will you send me leads or just a listing?

A listing, honestly. Customers land on your city page while pricing a repair and contact you directly — we don't sit in the middle, take a cut, or route the call. Your phone number is your phone number.

Something's wrong with my listing.

Email info@repairrange.io and we'll fix it. Same for removing it — no forms, no notice period.
