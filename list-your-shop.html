Questions

Common questions from shop owners

What happens after the free month?

We'll email you before it ends. If you'd like to continue, you choose a plan and pay via a simple payment link. If not, your listing is removed. No auto-billing, no surprises.

Do you take a commission on my repairs?

No. The listing fee is flat — $29, $49, or $89 depending on the plan. Every customer who contacts you through the listing is 100% yours. We don't track calls, take commissions, or insert ourselves between you and your customers.

Can I change or cancel my listing?

Yes — email us anytime to update your details or remove your listing. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Plans simply don't auto-renew; you choose to renew when the period ends.

Who runs RepairRange?

RepairRange is built and maintained by Khalil, the owner of Mayfield Phone Repair in Newcastle. He's a working phone repair technician — not a marketing company. The site is built on real bench experience.

Is the listing a "dofollow" backlink?

On the Verified and Featured plans, yes — your website link is a dofollow backlink from repairrange.io. This provides genuine SEO value for your own site's Google rankings.

Need shop management software too?
