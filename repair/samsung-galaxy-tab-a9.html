The common Galaxy Tab A9 repairs are priced flat — screen $150, battery and charging port $120–$150 — with same-day turnaround. Water damage and software work are quoted to your device.

Galaxy Tab A9 screen replacement — one bonded assembly

The Galaxy Tab A9 uses a laminated display: the touch glass and the LCD are bonded into a single part. There’s no glass-only option — a cracked screen means fitting a complete display assembly, and on the Galaxy Tab A9 that’s a flat $150, usually same day. There’s no S Pen digitiser or fingerprint layer to complicate the job, which helps keep the repair simple. We fit the full assembly, test touch and display, and reseal the device.

Battery replacement

The Galaxy Tab A9 battery is glued into the chassis and sits under the display, so a battery replacement is a careful bench job rather than a quick swap — $120–$150, usually same day. If your tablet drops charge fast, shuts down early, or the screen is lifting (a sign of a swollen battery), stop using it and book it in — a swollen battery is a safety issue. We fit a fresh cell and reseal the device.

Charging, water damage & software

The Galaxy Tab A9 charges over USB-C. If it won’t charge or cables keep dropping out, it’s often the port or the cable — worth a quick check before assuming the worst, and a port repair is a flat $120–$150. Liquid damage is quoted to the device (power it off and bring it in for a proper clean — that beats rice every time). Software and restore work — a Google (FRP) lock after a reset, boot loops, or a failed update — is quoted to the job, usually turned around the same day.

Thinking of a DIY fix?

The Galaxy Tab A9 is sealed with a bonded screen and a glued-in battery, so a DIY screen or battery job needs heat, suction and the right pry tools — cracked assemblies and punctured batteries are common first-timer outcomes, and a punctured cell is a fire risk. If you do tackle it, take your time on the heat and adhesive; for anything beyond that, a proper bench setup pays for itself.

FAQ

Galaxy Tab A9 repair questions

How much does a Galaxy Tab A9 screen replacement cost?

A Galaxy Tab A9 screen is a single bonded display assembly — the glass and LCD are one part — replaced flat at $150, usually same day. There’s no glass-only option because the layers are laminated together.

Can you replace just the glass on a Galaxy Tab A9?

No. The Galaxy Tab A9’s display is laminated, so the touch glass and panel come as one bonded part and are replaced together. Splitting glass from the panel costs more than a full assembly and rarely holds up, so a complete screen is the reliable fix.

Can the Galaxy Tab A9 battery be replaced?

Yes — $120–$150, usually same day. The battery is glued in under the display, so it’s a careful bench job, but a fresh cell restores full battery life.

Is it worth repairing a Galaxy Tab A9?

Usually yes for a screen or battery — a $150 screen or $120–$150 battery is a fraction of a new tablet. Because the Galaxy Tab A9 is a budget device, we’ll give you an honest steer if a repair doesn’t stack up against replacing it.

Do you offer quick turnaround?

Same-day turnaround on screen, battery and charging-port repairs where parts are in stock. Water damage and software work are quoted to the device, and you’ll get a turnaround estimate with your quote.
