FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 17 Air screen?

At an independent shop, an iPhone 17 Air screen replacement typically costs $179–$479 AUD. Apple charges approximately $329. The Air's ultra-thin construction adds labour time compared with the standard 17, pushing pricing closer to Pro-tier repairs.

Is the iPhone 17 Air more expensive to repair than the standard iPhone 17?

Yes, modestly. The titanium frame and ultra-thin 5.5mm chassis mean tighter tolerances and more careful disassembly, which adds to independent labour pricing across every repair category compared with the standard 17's aluminium body.

Can I replace the iPhone 17 Air battery myself?

It's possible with a DIY kit ($35–$59 for parts) but harder than on the standard 17 — the slimmer body leaves less clearance around the battery cell, so patience and the right adhesive-release tools matter more than usual.

How long does an iPhone 17 Air repair take?

Independent shop: roughly 55 minutes for a screen or battery job — slightly longer than the standard 17 due to the tighter internal fit. Apple-authorised service: same-day to 3 business days depending on part stock.
