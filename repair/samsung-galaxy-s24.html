DIY note

The S24 is one of the more approachable Samsung flagships to DIY — flat 6.2" display, aluminium frame, standard pry-and-heat, two years of community teardown guides. The under-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor still requires careful handling, and budget aftermarket panels may break it entirely. Quality aftermarket S24 panels have a strong 2026 track record — ask for OEM-equivalent or service-pack tier. For more on screen quality tiers, see our screen quality guide.

FAQ

Galaxy S24 repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy S24 screen?

At an independent repair shop, a Samsung Galaxy S24 screen replacement typically costs $199–$289 AUD. Authorised Samsung service runs $329–$449. The 6.2-inch flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X is one of the cheapest current-gen Samsung flagship screens to replace — two years on the market means aftermarket parts are widely available at every quality tier.

Why does the under-display fingerprint sensor matter for screen repairs?

The S24 uses an under-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor. After any screen replacement it has to recalibrate, and budget aftermarket panels often won't work with it at all. By 2026 quality aftermarket S24 panels have a strong track record; budget aftermarket still varies. For a deep dive, see our fingerprint sensor guide.

When is it worth replacing the Galaxy S24 battery?

A Galaxy S24 battery replacement at an independent shop costs $79–$119 AUD. Two years in, early S24 units are entering the zone where battery degradation becomes noticeable — most units sit at 85–90% health. If you're a heavy user charging twice a day, replacement now buys you another 18–24 months of full-day battery life. For the full framework, see our battery health guide.

Should I fix a cracked back on a Galaxy S24?

Back glass replacement costs $129–$199 AUD at independent shops. Cosmetic only — fix it before selling or trading in, and to stop dust ingress through the cracks.

Authorised service or an independent shop for the Galaxy S24?

For an out-of-warranty S24, an independent shop is almost always the better choice — 30–40% cheaper and faster. Choose authorised if you're inside warranty or have Samsung Care+. Be specific about part grade (genuine service pack vs OEM-quality aftermarket vs budget) before you commit.

Can I repair the Galaxy S24 myself?

The S24 is moderately DIY-friendly — flat 6.2-inch display, aluminium frame, well-documented teardown. The under-display fingerprint sensor still requires careful handling. DIY screen parts run $109–$169 plus tools. Two years of community guides make this one of the more approachable Samsung flagships to DIY.

How long does a Galaxy S24 screen repair take?

Independent shop: 30–60 minutes if the part is in stock — most shops keep S24 panels on the shelf by 2026. Authorised: 1–5 business days. DIY: 2–3 hours for a first attempt.
