iPad (11th gen, A16) screen replacement

The 11th-gen iPad uses an 11-inch Liquid Retina LCD (a 60Hz IPS panel). As the entry iPad, its glass and display aren't fully laminated, so we'll confirm on inspection whether the fix is the glass layer, the display, or both. Touch ID is built into the top power button and carried over during the repair. As a current model, screen parts are new and pricing is confirmed by quote.

Battery replacement

The battery is glued into the chassis under the display, so replacement is a careful job. If your iPad drops charge fast, shuts down early, or the screen is lifting (a swollen battery), stop using it and bring it in — a swollen battery is a safety issue. We fit a fresh cell and reseal the device.

Charging, water damage & everything else

The 11th-gen iPad charges over USB-C. Won't charge or accessories dropping out? It's often the port or cable — worth a free check before assuming the worst. We also handle liquid damage, rear-housing damage, cameras, buttons and speakers.

Thinking of a DIY fix?

iPads are sealed, thin and heavily glued, which makes DIY screen and battery jobs genuinely risky — cracked digitisers and punctured batteries are common first-timer outcomes. For anything beyond a case swap, a proper bench setup with heat, suction and the right tools pays for itself.
