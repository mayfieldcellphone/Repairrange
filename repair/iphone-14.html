How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 14 screen?
At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 14 screen replacement typically costs $199–$289 AUD. Authorised service is significantly more at $329–$449. The DIY route runs $99–$159 for the part plus the right tools, but the difficulty is rated moderate on this model.
