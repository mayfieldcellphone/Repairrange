How much does phone repair cost in Australia?

Most phones have more than one screen price, because they have more than one kind of screen. Incell, aftermarket OLED, refurbished original · each is a different part at a different price. Here is what each costs, for every model, next to what the manufacturer charges.

All prices are 2026 AUD, GST inclusive. The low end of each range is verified retail from an independent repair shop in Newcastle, NSW. The top of each range is an estimate, not a survey. Maker is the manufacturer's published out-of-warranty price. A dash means that part tier does not exist for that phone — most Android handsets have no budget screen option at all. Models with Full guide have detailed repair pages with FAQs, common issues, and DIY difficulty ratings. Models with View pricing have a pricing overview page with repair costs and key advice. Screen repairs

$120–$740

What you pay depends far more on the part than on the phone. An iPhone 17 Pro Max starts at $250 with an incell panel and $450 with a refurbished original. An iPhone SE starts at $120. Which tier should you choose? →

Battery repairs

$90–$200

Battery price barely moves with model age, because you aren't paying for the cell — you're paying for the risk of opening the phone. The technician goes in through the display. When to replace →

Back glass repairs

$279–$899

Those are Apple's prices. It's $279 on current models, but $899 on an iPhone 14 Pro Max and $889 on a 13 Pro Max — because before the iPhone 16, Apple replaced the entire chassis rather than the glass. An independent shop with a laser does not.
