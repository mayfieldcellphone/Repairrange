How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 13 Pro Max screen?
At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 13 Pro Max screen replacement typically costs $249–$379 AUD. Authorised Apple service is significantly more at around $399–$549. The 6.7-inch ProMotion OLED is one of the priciest panels of its generation — always confirm the part quality and warranty before you commit.
