FAQ

Galaxy S23+ repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy S23+ screen?

At an independent repair shop, a Samsung Galaxy S23+ screen replacement typically costs $289–$399 AUD. Authorised Samsung service runs $399–$549. The 6.6-inch flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X sits between the smaller S23 ($249–$359) and the curved S23 Ultra ($329–$469). Three years on the market means parts supply is mature and pricing has stabilised.

Why does the under-display fingerprint sensor matter for screen repairs?

The S23+ uses an under-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor — same approach Samsung has used since the S10. After any screen replacement it has to recalibrate, and budget aftermarket panels often won't work with it at all. By 2026 quality aftermarket S23+ panels have a strong track record; budget aftermarket still varies.

When is it worth replacing the Galaxy S23+ battery?

A Galaxy S23+ battery replacement at an independent shop costs $79–$119 AUD. Three years in, the 4,700mAh battery is firmly in degradation territory for most units — typical health sits at 80–85%. If you're charging more than once a day, replacement is one of the highest-value repairs on this generation.

Should I fix a cracked back on a Galaxy S23+?

Back glass replacement costs $149–$239 AUD at independent shops. Cosmetic only — fix it before selling or trading in, and to stop dust ingress through the cracks.

Authorised service or an independent shop for the Galaxy S23+?

For a 3-year-old phone like the S23+, an independent shop is almost always the better choice. Authorised service costs 40–50% more on a model where aftermarket parts are now mature and reliable. Save authorised for warranty situations — for the out-of-warranty S23+ in 2026, independent is the sensible call.

Can I repair the Galaxy S23+ myself?

The S23+ is moderately DIY-friendly — a flat 6.6-inch display, aluminium frame, well-documented teardown by now (three years of iFixit guides and YouTube walkthroughs). The under-display fingerprint sensor still requires care. DIY screen parts run $129–$199 plus tools. With three years of community knowledge, this is one of the better Samsung models to learn on.

How long does a Galaxy S23+ screen repair take?

Independent shop: 30–60 minutes if the part is in stock — by 2026 most shops keep S23+ panels on the shelf. Authorised: 1–5 business days. DIY: 2–3 hours for a first attempt.
