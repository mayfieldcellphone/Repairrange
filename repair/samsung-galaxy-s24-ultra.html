FAQ

Galaxy S24 Ultra repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy S24 Ultra screen?

Independent shop: $299–$499 AUD. Authorised: $499–$699. DIY: $199–$279. Curved Dynamic AMOLED is unforgiving — rated hard difficulty. The S24 Ultra is the most expensive mainstream flagship to fix.

When is it worth replacing the Galaxy S24 Ultra battery?

A Galaxy S24 Ultra battery replacement at an independent shop costs $99–$149 AUD. If battery health drops or you're charging more than once a day, replacement pays for itself in weeks.

Should I fix a cracked back on a Galaxy S24 Ultra?

Back glass replacement costs $179–$299 AUD. This is a hard repair (heat-and-pry required). Cosmetic only — skip unless selling.

Can I repair the Galaxy S24 Ultra myself?

Curved Dynamic AMOLED is unforgiving. Heat-and-pry is required for the back. Most expensive flagship to fix on the market. We don't recommend DIY for the screen unless experienced.

How long does a Galaxy S24 Ultra screen repair take?

Independent shop: 45–90 minutes (curved screens take longer than flat ones). Authorised: 1–5 business days. DIY: 3–4 hours for a first attempt.
