FAQ

iPhone 15 Pro Max repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 15 Pro Max screen?

At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 15 Pro Max screen replacement typically costs $329–$459 AUD. Authorised service is significantly more at $499–$649. The DIY route runs $180–$250 for the part plus the right tools, but the difficulty is rated hard on this model.

When is it worth replacing the iPhone 15 Pro Max battery?

An iPhone 15 Pro Max battery replacement at an independent shop costs $89–$139 AUD. If your battery health drops below 80% and you're charging more than once a day, replacement pays for itself in convenience within weeks.

Should I fix a cracked back on an iPhone 15 Pro Max?

Back glass replacement costs $169–$279 AUD at independent shops — significantly cheaper than older iPhones because this is the first generation with truly user-serviceable back glass. If you plan to trade in or sell, fixing the back returns roughly 60–80% of the cost in resale value.

Authorised service or an independent shop for the iPhone 15 Pro Max?

Independent shops are typically 30–50% cheaper and faster, but quality varies. Choose authorised service if: the device is still under warranty, you have AppleCare, or features like Face ID calibration are critical to you. Choose an independent shop if: cost matters, the device is out of warranty, and the shop has documented reviews.

Can I repair the iPhone 15 Pro Max myself?

This is the first iPhone with truly user-serviceable back glass. Apple's self-service repair program lists this model. If you do attempt the screen yourself, expect to spend $180–$250 on parts and tools, and budget for a second attempt if it's your first time.

How long does an iPhone 15 Pro Max screen repair take?

At a competent independent shop: typically 30–60 minutes if they have the part on hand. Authorised service is usually 1–3 business days for in-store, or longer if mailed in. DIY is best estimated as a 2–3 hour job for a first attempt — budget time, not just money.
