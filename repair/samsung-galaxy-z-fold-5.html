DIY note

We don't recommend DIY for the inner screen or hinge on any foldable — both involve specialist lamination and torque tools most home kits don't include, and a failed attempt usually costs more to fix than the original repair. The outer cover screen is DIY-feasible for an experienced repairer with the right heat and pry tools.

FAQ

Galaxy Z Fold 5 repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy Z Fold 5 inner screen?

Independent shop: $499–$799 AUD. Authorised Samsung service: $899–$1,199. This is the most expensive single-screen repair on the current market — the inner panel is a custom laminated assembly, not a standalone drop-in part.

Is it worth fixing the hinge instead of trading in the phone?

Usually yes. A hinge assembly replacement runs $299–$449 independently — well under trade-in loss on a device this expensive new. Only consider trading in if the inner screen and hinge both need work at the same time, since that combined bill can approach half the phone's original price.

Should I fix a cracked outer screen on a Galaxy Z Fold 5?

Outer cover screen replacement costs $249–$379 AUD — significantly cheaper than the inner panel and a much more common repair since it's the display you actually drop the phone on. Worth fixing promptly, since a cracked cover screen can shed glass into the hinge mechanism.

Can I repair the Galaxy Z Fold 5 myself?

We don't recommend DIY for the inner screen or hinge — both require specialist lamination and torque equipment. The outer cover screen is more approachable for an experienced DIYer, with parts running $179–$259.

How long does a Galaxy Z Fold 5 inner screen repair take?

Independent shop: typically 2–4 hours, and many shops need to order the part in first — call ahead to check stock. Authorised: 3–7 business days, sometimes longer if the unit needs to go to a regional service centre.
