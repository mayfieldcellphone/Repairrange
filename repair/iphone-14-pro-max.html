How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 14 Pro Max screen?
At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 14 Pro Max screen replacement typically costs $309–$469 AUD. Authorised Apple service is significantly more at around $449–$629. The 6.7-inch ProMotion OLED with Dynamic Island is one of the most expensive panels to replace — always confirm the part quality and warranty before you commit.
