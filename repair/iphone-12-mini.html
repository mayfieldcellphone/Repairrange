Bench Notes

01

Small chassis, tight tolerances. The 12 mini packs the same internal architecture as the standard 12 into a noticeably smaller shell, so battery and screen swaps require more care around the tightly packed antenna lines and speaker assembly.

02

Battery health drops faster than larger 12-series models. The smaller cell has less headroom, so owners often notice degradation sooner — many are due for a battery swap by the 3-year mark.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 12 mini screen?

At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 12 mini screen replacement typically costs $95–$170 AUD. Authorised Apple service runs $349–$419. Because the mini sold in lower volumes than the standard 12, some budget shops carry it as a special-order part — confirm stock before booking.

Is it worth repairing an iPhone 12 mini in 2026?

Yes, especially for battery and screen jobs — both come in well under $200 and the mini remains one of the only genuinely compact 5G phones on the market, which keeps resale and personal-use value high for people who prefer the smaller form factor.

Are parts for the iPhone 12 mini harder to find?

A little. The mini sold in lower numbers than the other 12-series models, so some smaller independent shops may need a day or two to source a screen or back glass rather than having it on the shelf. Battery and charging port parts remain widely stocked.
