How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 15 screen?

At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 15 screen replacement typically costs $99–$349 AUD. Authorised service is significantly more at $369–$499. The DIY route runs $129–$189 for the part plus the right tools, with moderate difficulty on this model.
