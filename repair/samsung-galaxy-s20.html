DIY note

The S20's curved-edge display makes it a moderate-to-hard DIY job — the edges are prone to cracking during removal even with careful heat application. The under-display fingerprint sensor needs care. Six years of community guides help, but genuine parts are increasingly scarce; budget on quality aftermarket. DIY screen parts run $62–$99 plus tools.

FAQ

Galaxy S20 repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy S20 screen?

At an independent repair shop, a Samsung Galaxy S20 screen replacement typically costs $139–$199 AUD. Authorised Samsung service runs $219–$289, though official parts and service can be harder to access on a phone this age.

Is it still worth repairing a Galaxy S20 in 2026?

For a battery or screen repair, usually yes — both are well under $200. At six years old, this is a judgment call worth weighing against Android update support windows and general performance, but the repair itself remains inexpensive.

When is it worth replacing the Galaxy S20 battery?

A Galaxy S20 battery replacement at an independent shop costs $59–$79 AUD. At six years old, almost every unit still on its original battery is well past the point where a swap makes a dramatic difference to daily use.

Are parts for the Galaxy S20 getting harder to find?

Somewhat. Genuine Samsung parts get scarcer this far past release, and most independent shops now rely on aftermarket supply for the S20. Battery and screen parts remain widely available; back glass and specialty components may take a little longer to source.

Can I repair the Galaxy S20 myself?

The curved-edge display makes this a moderate-to-hard DIY job — edges crack easily during removal. Battery and USB-C swaps are more approachable. DIY screen parts run $62–$99 plus tools and heat equipment.
