MacBook Air (Intel) screen replacement — $350–$650

The MacBook Air (Intel) screen is a single lid assembly, so a replacement is more involved than swapping a phone panel. Cracked glass, lines or flicker, black patches and hinge faults all fall here. Where damage is limited to the glass the lower end applies; a full genuine assembly sits toward the top.

Battery replacement — $150–$300

If your Mac won't hold charge, swells, shuts down early, or macOS flags "Service Recommended", the battery is the fix. The cell is bonded into the chassis, so most of the cost is careful removal and safe fitting, not the battery itself. Price includes labour and a charging health check.

Software & macOS repair — $100–$250

Won't boot past the Apple logo, kernel panics, stuck updates, forgotten-password lockouts, or a machine suddenly crawling — these are software jobs. Day-to-day issues sit at the lower end; deeper recovery or reinstalls sit higher. Wherever possible the work is data-safe; if your data is at risk we tell you before touching anything.

Liquid & water damage — by quote

If liquid has just been spilled: shut the machine down and do not charge it. Powering on a wet board turns a recoverable spill into a dead logic board. Liquid damage can't be priced sight unseen, so it starts with a diagnostic, then a clear quote for corrosion cleaning or board-level work. Many are recoverable; it depends on what the liquid touched and how fast it was switched off.

Common questions

How much does a MacBook Air (Intel) screen replacement cost?

A MacBook Air (Intel) screen replacement typically ranges from $350–$650 depending on the display assembly and the exact damage. Get a precise quote before you book.

How much is a battery replacement?

Around $150–$300 including labour, reflecting the careful removal of a bonded-in cell.

Can a water-damaged MacBook Air (Intel) be fixed?

Often yes, but it's assessed case by case — power it off, don't charge it, and bring it in for a diagnostic.

How long does a repair take?

Most screen, battery and software jobs are same or next day; liquid work depends on the diagnostic.
