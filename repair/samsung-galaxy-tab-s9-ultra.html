Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra screen replacement — one bonded assembly

The Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra uses a laminated display: the touch glass and the AMOLED panel are bonded into a single part. There’s no glass-only option — a cracked screen means fitting a complete AMOLED assembly, and on the Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra the 14.6-inch AMOLED assembly is the single most expensive part of the tablet — often around two-thirds of the device’s value — so it’s quoted to your exact device rather than a flat price. The S Pen digitiser is part of the display stack, so it’s replaced together with the screen. We fit the full assembly, test touch and display, and reseal the device.

Battery replacement

The Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra battery is glued into the chassis and sits under the display, so a battery replacement is a careful bench job rather than a quick swap — quoted to the job. On an AMOLED tablet the battery sits under a bonded panel that can crack if forced, so we quote battery and charging-port work to the device and price in that risk rather than flat-rating it. A swollen battery is a safety issue — stop using the tablet and book it in. We fit a fresh cell and reseal the device.

Charging, water damage & software

The Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra charges over USB-C. If it won’t charge or cables keep dropping out, it’s often the port or the cable — worth a quick check before assuming the worst, and a port repair is quoted to the device (see the note on AMOLED risk above). Liquid damage is quoted to the device (power it off and bring it in for a proper clean — that beats rice every time). Software and restore work — a Google (FRP) lock after a reset, boot loops, or a failed update — is quoted to the job, usually turned around the same day.

Thinking of a DIY fix?

The Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra is sealed with a bonded screen and a glued-in battery, so a DIY screen or battery job needs heat, suction and the right pry tools — cracked assemblies and punctured batteries are common first-timer outcomes, and a punctured cell is a fire risk. The S Pen layer and, on some models, an under-display fingerprint reader add fragile parts to the stack, so there’s more to get wrong than on a plain LCD tablet. If you do tackle it, take your time on the heat and adhesive; for anything beyond that, a proper bench setup pays for itself.

FAQ

Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra repair questions

How much does a Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra screen replacement cost?

The Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra uses a large 14.6-inch AMOLED assembly, which is often around two-thirds of the tablet’s value. We quote it to your exact device rather than flat-rating it, so you get a precise price before any work starts.

Can you replace just the glass on a Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra?

No. The Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra’s display is laminated, so the touch glass and panel come as one bonded part and are replaced together. Splitting glass from the panel costs more than a full assembly and rarely holds up, so a complete screen is the reliable fix.

Can the Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra battery be replaced?

Yes, but we quote it to the device. The battery sits under a bonded AMOLED panel that can crack if forced, so we price in that risk rather than flat-rating it. A fresh cell restores full battery life.

Is it worth repairing a Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra?

Almost always — the Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra is a $1,500–$3,000 tablet, so even a by-quote screen is typically far cheaper than replacing it. We quote exactly and give you an honest steer before any work starts.

Do you offer quick turnaround?

Screen repairs are usually same day where the panel is in stock; battery, charging-port and board work are quoted with a turnaround estimate. Water damage and software work are quoted to the device.
