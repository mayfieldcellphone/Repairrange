iPad mini (6th gen) screen replacement

The iPad mini (6th gen) uses a fully laminated Liquid Retina LCD, where the glass and display are bonded together. A cracked screen is replaced as a single glass-and-LCD assembly. Because the glass and display are laminated together, a cracked screen is replaced as one assembly. Touch ID is built into the top power button, which is paired to the device and carried over during the repair to keep fingerprint unlock working.

Screen work is the most common iPad repair we see, usually from a drop onto a corner. Bring it in and we'll assess whether it's glass, display or both, and quote it on the spot.

Battery replacement

iPad batteries are glued into the chassis and sit under the display assembly, so a battery replacement on the iPad mini is a careful, time-consuming job rather than a quick swap. If your iPad mini (6th gen) drops charge fast, shuts down early, or the screen is lifting (a sign of a swollen battery), stop using it and book it in — a swollen battery is a safety issue. We fit a fresh cell and reseal the device.

Charging, water damage & everything else

This 2021 model charges over USB-C. If it won't charge or accessories keep dropping out, it's often the port or the cable — worth a free check before assuming the worst. We also handle liquid damage (the sooner the better — powered-off and in for a clean beats rice every time), rear-housing damage, cameras, buttons and speakers.

iPads are sealed, thin and heavily glued, which makes DIY screen and battery jobs genuinely risky — cracked digitisers and punctured batteries are common first-timer outcomes. For anything beyond a case swap, a proper bench setup pays for itself.
