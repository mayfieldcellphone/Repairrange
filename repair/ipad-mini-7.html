iPad mini (A17 Pro) screen replacement

The iPad mini (A17 Pro) uses a fully laminated 8.3-inch Liquid Retina LCD, where the glass and display are bonded together, so a cracked screen is replaced as one glass-and-LCD assembly. Touch ID is built into the top power button and is paired to the device — it's carried over during the repair to keep fingerprint unlock working. Screen work is the most common iPad mini repair we see, usually after a drop onto a corner.

Battery replacement

iPad batteries are glued into the chassis and sit under the display, so a battery replacement on the mini is a careful, time-consuming job rather than a quick swap. If your mini drops charge fast, shuts down early, or the screen is lifting (a sign of a swollen battery), stop using it and book it in — a swollen battery is a safety issue. We fit a fresh cell and reseal the device.

Charging, water damage & everything else

The A17 Pro mini charges over USB-C. If it won't charge or accessories keep dropping out, it's often the port or the cable — worth a free check before assuming the worst. We also handle liquid damage (powered-off and in for a clean beats rice every time), rear-housing damage, cameras, buttons and speakers.

Thinking of a DIY fix?

iPads are sealed, thin and heavily glued, which makes DIY screen and battery jobs genuinely risky — cracked digitisers and punctured batteries are common first-timer outcomes. For anything beyond a case swap, a proper bench setup with heat, suction and the right tools pays for itself.
