FAQ

Galaxy A57 repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy A57 screen?

Early independent-shop quotes put a Galaxy A57 screen replacement at roughly $169–$229 AUD. Authorised Samsung service runs $259–$329. These are early-market estimates and should settle as parts supply catches up through 2026.

Is the A57 worth fixing over buying a new phone?

Yes. As a phone that just launched at $549.99, any of these repairs comes in well under the cost of replacement. There's no reason to consider upgrading over a screen or battery repair this early in the phone's life.

When should I replace the battery?

If battery health has dropped below 80% or the phone doesn't make it through a full day. Independent shops charge roughly $72–$102 for this, and it's a beginner-friendly job.

Can I repair the Galaxy A57 myself?

Battery and USB-C port swaps should be beginner-friendly, in line with the rest of the A-series. Screen and back glass are moderate difficulty — worth waiting for dedicated tool kits to mature over the next few months if you're new to phone repair.

How does the A57 compare to the A56 for repair cost?

Early estimates put the A57 modestly above the A56 across the board, consistent with its higher launch price and upgraded display and chipset. Expect independent pricing to converge as the device matures.
