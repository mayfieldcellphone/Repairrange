FAQ

Galaxy S23 repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy S23 screen?

At an independent repair shop, a Samsung Galaxy S23 screen replacement typically costs $249\u2013$359 AUD. Authorised Samsung service runs $379\u2013$499. The 6.1-inch flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X is the cheapest S-series screen replacement after the smaller A-series models. Three-year-old units now have firmly-established aftermarket part supply at every quality tier.

Why does the under-display fingerprint sensor matter for screen repairs?

The S23 uses an under-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor \u2014 same approach Samsung has used since the S10. After any screen replacement it has to recalibrate, and budget aftermarket panels often won't work with it at all. By 2026 the quality aftermarket S23 panels have a strong track record with the fingerprint sensor; budget aftermarket still varies.

When is it worth replacing the Galaxy S23 battery?

A Galaxy S23 battery replacement at an independent shop costs $79\u2013$119 AUD. Three years in, S23 batteries are firmly in degradation territory \u2014 most units now sit at 80\u201385% health under normal use. If you're charging more than once a day, replacement is one of the highest-value repairs on this generation.

Should I fix a cracked back on a Galaxy S23?

Back glass replacement costs $139\u2013$229 AUD at independent shops. Cosmetic only \u2014 but fix it before you sell or trade in, and stop dust ingress through the cracks.

Authorised service or an independent shop for the Galaxy S23?

For a 3-year-old phone like the S23, an independent shop is almost always the better choice. Authorised service costs 40\u201350% more on a model where aftermarket parts are now mature and reliable. Save authorised service for warranty or premium-flagship situations \u2014 for the standard S23 in 2026, independent is the sensible call.

Can I repair the Galaxy S23 myself?

The S23 is moderately DIY-friendly \u2014 a flat 6.1-inch display, aluminium frame, well-documented teardown by now (three years of iFixit guides and YouTube walkthroughs). The under-display fingerprint sensor still requires care. DIY screen parts run $109\u2013$179 plus tools. With three years of community knowledge, this is one of the better Samsung models to learn on.

How long does a Galaxy S23 screen repair take?

Independent shop: 30\u201360 minutes if the part is in stock \u2014 by 2026 most shops keep S23 panels on the shelf. Authorised: 1\u20135 business days. DIY: 2\u20133 hours for a first attempt.
