DIY note

The S25+ is more approachable than the S25 Ultra — flat display, aluminium frame (not titanium), no S Pen complications. But the same Samsung-specific risk applies: the under-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor must be preserved during the screen swap. Budget aftermarket panels may break the fingerprint sensor entirely — genuine service-pack screens preserve it, quality aftermarket usually does, budget often doesn't. Confirm the part grade with your supplier before ordering.

FAQ

Galaxy S25+ repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked Galaxy S25+ screen?

At an independent repair shop, a Samsung Galaxy S25+ screen replacement typically costs $329–$449 AUD. Authorised Samsung service runs $449–$599. The 6.7-inch flat Dynamic AMOLED 2X is cheaper to replace than the Ultra's larger curved panel, but more expensive than the standard S25 — confirm part grade before committing.

Why does the under-display fingerprint sensor matter for screen repairs?

The S25+ uses an under-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor that lives behind the screen. After any screen replacement it has to recalibrate, and budget aftermarket panels often won't work with it at all — you'll lose biometric unlock entirely. This is the Samsung equivalent of Face ID on iPhones. Genuine service-pack screens preserve it; quality OEM-equivalent aftermarket usually does; budget aftermarket often doesn't.

When is it worth replacing the Galaxy S25+ battery?

A Galaxy S25+ battery replacement at an independent shop costs $89–$129 AUD. The S25+ carries a 4,900mAh battery driving a 6.7-inch always-on display. If you're charging more than once a day, replacement pays for itself within weeks.

Should I fix a cracked back on a Galaxy S25+?

Back glass replacement costs $139–$209 AUD at independent shops. The S25+ uses an aluminium frame (not titanium like the Ultra) which keeps the labour fractionally lower. Cosmetic only — fix it before selling or trading in.

Authorised service or an independent shop for the Galaxy S25+?

Independent shops are typically 30–40% cheaper and faster than Samsung authorised service. Choose authorised if you're inside warranty or have Samsung Care+. For an out-of-warranty S25+, a reputable independent shop is the sensible choice — be specific about part grade (genuine service pack vs OEM-quality aftermarket vs budget).

Can I repair the Galaxy S25+ myself?

The S25+ is moderately hard to DIY — the flat display is more forgiving than the curved Ultra screens of past generations, but the under-display fingerprint sensor still requires careful handling and the chassis adhesive is strong. DIY screen parts run $149–$229 plus tools. Budget aftermarket panels may break the fingerprint sensor entirely.

How long does a Galaxy S25+ screen repair take?

Independent shop: 45–75 minutes if the part is in stock. Authorised: 1–5 business days. DIY: 2–3 hours for a first attempt.
