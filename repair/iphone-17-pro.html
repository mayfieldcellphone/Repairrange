Face ID warning

Face ID is paired to the logic board. Apple authorised service and quality independent shops using Apple's self-service repair tools can preserve Face ID during a screen replacement. Budget shops that skip the pairing process will break it permanently — and once lost, it cannot be restored without Apple's tools. Ask about Face ID preservation before committing to any screen replacement.

FAQ

iPhone 17 Pro repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 17 Pro screen?

At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 17 Pro screen replacement typically costs $115–$449 AUD. Apple's out-of-warranty price is approximately $339. The smaller 6.3" panel is slightly cheaper than the Pro Max's 6.9" screen.

Will Face ID work after a screen replacement?

Face ID uses a TrueDepth camera module paired to the logic board. Apple authorised service and quality independent shops using Apple's self-service tools can preserve it. Budget shops that skip pairing will break it permanently. Ask before you commit.

When is it worth replacing the iPhone 17 Pro battery?

Battery replacement costs $89–$139 AUD at independent shops (Apple charges $119). Less than a year old, so most units are above 95% health. Replacement becomes relevant at year 2–3. See our battery health guide.

Apple or independent shop for the iPhone 17 Pro?

Apple's pricing on the 17 Pro is competitive — ~$339 for screen, ~$139 for back glass, $119 for battery. Choose Apple for guaranteed Face ID preservation. Choose independent for faster turnaround or USB-C port repair (which Apple doesn't offer standalone).

Can I repair the iPhone 17 Pro myself?

Moderately difficult. Apple's self-service repair program supports the 17 Pro with genuine parts. DIY screen parts run $180–$270 plus tool rental. Face ID can be preserved through Apple's process. Third-party parts are cheaper but risk Face ID loss.

How long does an iPhone 17 Pro screen repair take?

Independent shop: 30–60 minutes. Apple Store: same-day if parts are available, otherwise 3–5 business days. DIY: 2–3 hours for a first attempt.
