Face ID warning

Face ID on iPhones is paired to the logic board. This is the iPhone equivalent of the Samsung fingerprint sensor issue — but more locked down. If the TrueDepth camera module is damaged or disconnected during a screen replacement, Face ID is lost permanently on non-authorised repairs. Apple authorised service can re-pair the module; most quality independent shops using Apple's self-service repair tools can too. Budget shops that don't use the pairing process will break Face ID. Ask specifically about Face ID preservation before committing to any iPhone 17 Pro Max screen replacement.

FAQ

iPhone 17 Pro Max repair questions, answered.

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 17 Pro Max screen?

At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 17 Pro Max screen replacement typically costs $349–$549 AUD depending on part grade. Apple's own out-of-warranty screen repair is $379 AUD — unusually competitive with independents because Apple subsidises screen repairs. If Face ID preservation matters to you (and it should), Apple's authorised service is worth considering.

Will Face ID work after a screen replacement?

Face ID on the iPhone 17 Pro Max uses a TrueDepth camera module that's paired to the logic board. Apple authorised service preserves Face ID because they can re-pair the module. Most quality independent shops can also preserve it using Apple's self-service repair tools. Budget repair shops may not — and once Face ID is lost on a non-authorised repair, it cannot be restored. Ask before you commit.

When is it worth replacing the iPhone 17 Pro Max battery?

An iPhone 17 Pro Max battery replacement at an independent shop costs $89–$149 AUD. Apple charges $119. The phone is less than a year old, so most units are still above 95% health. Battery replacement becomes relevant around year 2–3 when health drops below 80%. See our battery health guide for the full framework.

Apple or independent shop for the iPhone 17 Pro Max?

This is one of the few models where Apple's own pricing is genuinely competitive — $379 for screen, $159 for back glass, $119 for battery. Choose Apple if: you want guaranteed Face ID preservation, or the repair is under AppleCare+. Choose independent if: you need faster turnaround (same-day vs 3–5 days), or you need a repair Apple doesn't offer standalone (like USB-C port).

Can I repair the iPhone 17 Pro Max myself?

Moderately difficult. Apple's self-service repair program supports the iPhone 17 Pro Max with genuine parts and tools. DIY screen parts run $200–$300 plus a tool rental deposit. The titanium frame and Ceramic Shield glass are well-documented but require patience. Face ID can be preserved through Apple's self-service process. Third-party DIY parts are cheaper but risk Face ID loss.

How long does an iPhone 17 Pro Max screen repair take?

Independent shop: 30–60 minutes if the part is in stock. Apple Store / authorised: same-day if parts are available, otherwise 3–5 business days. DIY: 2–3 hours for a first attempt using Apple's self-service kit.
