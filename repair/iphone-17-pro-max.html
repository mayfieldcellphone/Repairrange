FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 17 Pro Max screen?
At a repair shop, an iPhone 17 Pro Max screen replacement typically costs $115–$449 AUD depending on part grade. Fitting it yourself with a SelfRepairKit starts at $115 for a Budget INCELL panel, $150 for Pro OLED, or $449 for a genuine Elite OEM display.

Can I replace the iPhone 17 Pro Max screen myself?
Yes. It takes most first-timers about 45 minutes. The critical step is transferring the Face ID module to the new screen without damaging it. A model-matched kit includes the pentalobe and tri-point drivers, ESD-safe tools, adhesive, and a step-by-step video guide.

Will a replacement screen break Face ID?
No — as long as you transfer the original Face ID dot-projector assembly across to the new display and don't damage it. Face ID is paired to the logic board, not to the screen itself.
