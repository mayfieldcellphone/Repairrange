FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to fix a cracked iPhone 14 Plus screen?
At an independent repair shop, an iPhone 14 Plus screen replacement typically costs $109–$369 AUD. Authorised service is significantly more at $349–$479. The DIY route runs $109–$169 for the part plus the right tools, with moderate difficulty on this model.

Is the iPhone 14 Plus more expensive to repair than the standard 14?
Yes, by roughly $10–$20 across screen, battery and back-glass jobs. The Plus uses a larger 6.7" panel and the same big battery cell as the Pro Max, both of which cost more as parts — the labour and difficulty are essentially identical to the standard iPhone 14.

Does a third-party screen affect Face ID or True Tone on the iPhone 14 Plus?
A quality aftermarket or Pro OLED screen shouldn't touch Face ID, since that's tied to the TrueDepth camera module, not the display. True Tone can show a "non-genuine display" warning after a swap unless the shop transfers the original display's microcontroller or uses a programmer to pair the new screen — ask before you book.
