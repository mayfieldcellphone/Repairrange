How to Reset a POCO M6 Pro.

Learn how to soft reset (reboot without losing files), factory reset via the Settings application, or hard reset your POCO M6 Pro using hardware button combinations inside Android Recovery mode.

🔒 Google FRP Lock Alert: Performing a hardware recovery reset will trigger the device verification lock. Ensure you know your accounts before resetting or follow our unlock solutions below.

This guide covers every reset method for the xiaomi poco M6 PRO running Android. Choose Soft Reset to reboot without losing data, Factory Reset to erase everything from Settings, or Hardware Recovery Reset using button combinations. After a hardware reset, Google FRP (Factory Reset Protection) requires your Google account credentials.

Soft Reset (Force Restart)

Step 1Hold Power + Volume Down for 10-15 seconds

Keep holding until the screen turns off and the device restarts.

Factory Reset via Settings

Step 1Settings -> System -> Reset Options

Navigate to system options.

Step 2Select "Factory Data Reset" or "Erase All Data"

Confirm with lock code.

Factory Reset via Recovery Mode

⚠️ Warning: Erases all user files. Google Factory Reset Protection (FRP) will trigger. You will need the logged-in Google account.

Step 1Power off completely

Wait 30 seconds.

Step 2Hold Power + Volume Down (or Volume Up)

Hold until the brand logo appears. Release.

Step 3Select "Wipe data/factory reset"

Use Volume keys to scroll and Power key to select.

Step 4Reboot system now

Restart device.

Pre-Reset Safety & Data Preservation Checklist

Before initiating a factory reset on your POCO M6 Pro, taking proactive steps to safeguard your irreplaceable personal data and security authenticators is vital. Once the internal NAND storage is wiped, file-based encryption keys are shredded permanently.

  • Google Account Cloud Sync: Go to Settings → System → Backup (or Settings → Google → Backup) and verify that Google Drive sync is completed for contacts, call history, and device settings.
  • Photo & Video Archival: Ensure Google Photos or your chosen cloud storage provider has finished synchronising full-resolution images and videos over Wi-Fi.
  • 2FA & Banking Authenticator Transfer: Open authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) and verify account cloud sync is enabled. Resetting your POCO M6 Pro without migrating 2FA keys can permanently disrupt access to Australian banking apps and myGov accounts.
  • SIM & MicroSD Ejection: Eject your physical SIM tray and any external MicroSD card before running recovery mode to ensure external media remains untouched.

Anti-Theft Lock Avoidance Protocol (Google FRP)

All modern Android smartphones running Android 5.1 Lollipop through Android 14 and 15 come equipped with Google Factory Reset Protection (FRP). When a hard reset is triggered via hardware button combinations inside Recovery Mode, the phone's bootloader marks the wipe as untrusted. Upon rebooting, the device will strictly require the Google Account username and password previously registered on the POCO M6 Pro.

How to Avoid FRP Lockout Before Resetting:

  1. Open Settings and scroll down to Accounts & Backup (or Passwords & Accounts).
  2. Tap Manage Accounts and select each Google account listed.
  3. Tap Remove Account and confirm with your device PIN or fingerprint.
  4. If selling or trading in the device, also remove any proprietary manufacturer accounts (such as Samsung Account, Mi Account, or HeyTap/Oppo ID).
  5. Once all accounts are removed, a factory reset will allow any new user to set up the phone with their own Google credentials without restriction.

Hardware Button Recovery & Deep Troubleshooting

Hardware recovery mode bypasses the operating system completely, communicating directly with the bootloader partition. Follow these technician guidelines for your POCO M6 Pro:

  • The "No Command" Screen Fix: If you see a fallen Android robot graphic with the words "No Command", do not panic. Hold down the Power button, press the Volume Up button once, and immediately release both. The full Android Recovery text menu will instantly display.
  • Wipe Cache Partition First: If you are resetting your POCO M6 Pro to resolve freezing, overheating, or battery drain, try selecting Wipe cache partition before doing a full factory wipe. Clearing the system cache resolves many software corruption issues without deleting personal photos, apps, or texts.
  • Exiting Recovery Safely: If you enter recovery mode by mistake and do not wish to erase your data, simply use the Volume keys to highlight Reboot system now and press the Power key. Your phone will reboot safely into standard Android without losing any files.

Post-Reset Setup & Performance Optimization

Once the wipe process finishes and your POCO M6 Pro restarts to the initial setup screen ("Hello" / "Welcome"), follow these steps for a clean, stable experience:

  • Initial Setup Configuration: Connect to a secure Wi-Fi network and sign in with your primary account. If you plan to sell or donate your phone, stop at the language selection screen and power off the device so the recipient can configure their own profile.
  • Fresh Install vs Full Cloud Restore: If you performed a reset to fix sluggish performance, high system storage usage, or battery drain, consider setting up as a New Device and manually downloading essential apps rather than restoring an entire system snapshot. Restoring old system backups often re-imports cached app corruption and rogue background processes.
  • Install All System Updates: After setup, immediately check Settings → System → Software Update (or Settings → General → Software Update). Installing the latest security patches ensures all operating system libraries are refreshed and patched against known vulnerabilities.
  • Hardware Diagnostics: Verify that touch responsiveness across the entire digitizer, cameras, ear speaker, and charging ports are functioning properly. If your phone still experiences crashes, frozen screens, or overheating after a full factory reset, the root cause is almost certainly physical hardware damage (such as logic board failure, power IC damage, or degraded battery chemistry) that requires bench inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions — POCO M6 Pro Reset

Will a factory reset erase the SIM card or eSIM on my POCO M6 Pro?

No. A standard factory reset or recovery mode wipe only erases internal flash storage (user apps, photos, messages, and settings). Physical SIM cards are completely untouched. For eSIM profiles, iOS and Android explicitly prompt you whether to keep or remove eSIM plans during the reset process. Choose 'Keep eSIM' to retain your mobile plan.

Can I recover my photos and files after a hard reset on POCO M6 Pro?

On modern smartphones featuring hardware-backed file-based encryption (FBE), performing a factory reset securely destroys the cryptographic master keys stored inside the Secure Enclave / Knox chipset. Without an existing cloud backup (iCloud, Google One, or PC backup), deleted files cannot be retrieved even by professional forensic data recovery tools.

Does a hard recovery reset remove Google FRP or iCloud Activation Lock?

No. Hardware recovery resets do NOT bypass Anti-Theft locks. Factory Reset Protection (FRP) and iCloud Activation Lock are stored in cloud security databases linked to the device hardware IMEI and serial number. The device will mandate entry of the original registered credentials upon booting up.

How do I reset my POCO M6 Pro if the touchscreen is completely unresponsive?

If the screen is cracked or the digitizer does not respond to touch, use the Hardware Button Recovery method detailed in this guide. Because navigation in Recovery Mode relies exclusively on physical Volume and Power keys, you can initiate a full Wipe Data / Factory Reset without needing touch screen input.

What should I do if my POCO M6 Pro is stuck in a bootloop after resetting?

If the device continually restarts or gets stuck on the brand logo after a wipe, force restart it and enter Recovery Mode again. Select 'Wipe cache partition' if available, or perform another 'Wipe data / factory reset'. If the issue persists, the operating system firmware may need to be re-flashed via official PC flashing software, or the motherboard/battery requires technician bench repair.
