How to Reset a iPhone 15.

Learn how to soft reset (reboot without losing files), factory reset via the Settings application, or hard reset your iPhone 15 using hardware button combinations inside iOS DFU (Device Firmware Update) mode.

🔒 Google FRP / iCloud Lock Alert: Performing a hardware recovery reset will trigger the device verification lock. Ensure you know your accounts before resetting or follow our unlock solutions below.

This guide covers every reset method for Apple Iphone 15 running iOS. Choose Soft Reset to reboot without losing data, Factory Reset to erase everything from Settings, or Hardware Recovery Reset to restore via DFU mode. After a hardware reset, Activation Lock requires your Apple ID credentials.

Soft Reset (Force Restart)

Step 1Quick-press Volume Up

Press and release the Volume Up button quickly.

Step 2Quick-press Volume Down

Press and release the Volume Down button quickly.

Step 3Hold Side button for 10-15 seconds

Keep holding the Side button until the Apple logo appears, then release. The phone restarts.

✅ Good to know: This does NOT erase data. It is the equivalent of turning the phone off and on again. Fixes frozen screens and software glitches.

Factory Reset via Settings (You know your passcode)

Step 1Open Settings -> General -> Transfer or Reset iPhone

Scroll to the bottom of the General settings list.

Step 2Tap "Erase All Content and Settings"

You will be prompted to enter your passcode and Apple ID password.

Step 3Confirm the erase

The phone erases completely and boots to the setup screen. Back up to iCloud first.

✅ Good to know: Requires your passcode and Apple ID credentials.

Factory Reset via Recovery Mode (Forgotten passcode)

⚠️ Warning: This erases ALL data. iCloud Activation Lock will trigger on startup. You must enter your original Apple ID credentials to reactivate.

Step 1Connect to a Mac or PC

Use a USB cable. Open Finder on Mac or iTunes on Windows.

Step 2Enter Recovery Mode

Quick-press Volume Up, quick-press Volume Down, then hold Side button until the recovery screen (computer & cable icon) appears. Do not release at the Apple logo.

Step 3Click "Restore" in Finder/iTunes

This downloads the latest iOS and erases the phone completely.
