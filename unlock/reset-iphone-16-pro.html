How to Reset a iPhone 16 Pro.

Learn how to soft reset (reboot without losing files), factory reset via the Settings application, or hard reset your iPhone 16 Pro using hardware button combinations inside iOS DFU (Device Firmware Update) mode.

🔒 Google FRP / iCloud Lock Alert: Performing a hardware recovery reset will trigger the device verification lock. Ensure you know your accounts before resetting or follow our unlock solutions below.

This guide covers every reset method for Apple Iphone 16 PRO running iOS. Choose Soft Reset to reboot without losing data, Factory Reset to erase everything from Settings, or Hardware Recovery Reset to restore via DFU mode. After a hardware reset, Activation Lock requires your Apple ID credentials.

Soft Reset (Force Restart)

Step 1Quick-press Volume Up

Press and release the Volume Up button quickly.

Step 2Quick-press Volume Down

Press and release the Volume Down button quickly.

Step 3Hold Side button for 10-15 seconds

Keep holding the Side button until the Apple logo appears, then release. The phone restarts.

✅ Good to know: This does NOT erase data. It is the equivalent of turning the phone off and on again. Fixes frozen screens and software glitches.

Factory Reset via Settings (You know your passcode)

Step 1Open Settings -> General -> Transfer or Reset iPhone

Scroll to the bottom of the General settings list.

Step 2Tap "Erase All Content and Settings"

You will be prompted to enter your passcode and Apple ID password.

Step 3Confirm the erase

The phone erases completely and boots to the setup screen. Back up to iCloud first.

✅ Good to know: Requires your passcode and Apple ID credentials.

Factory Reset via Recovery Mode (Forgotten passcode)

⚠️ Warning: This erases ALL data. iCloud Activation Lock will trigger on startup. You must enter your original Apple ID credentials to reactivate.

Step 1Connect to a Mac or PC

Use a USB cable. Open Finder on Mac or iTunes on Windows.

Step 2Enter Recovery Mode

Quick-press Volume Up, quick-press Volume Down, then hold Side button until the recovery screen (computer & cable icon) appears. Do not release at the Apple logo.

Step 3Click "Restore" in Finder/iTunes

This downloads the latest iOS and erases the phone completely.

Pre-Reset Safety & Data Preservation Checklist

Before initiating a factory reset on your iPhone 16 Pro, taking proactive steps to safeguard your irreplaceable personal data and security authenticators is vital. Once the internal NAND storage is wiped, file-based encryption keys are shredded permanently.

  • iCloud Backup: Connect to Wi-Fi, open Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup and tap Back Up Now. Verify that Photos, Messages, and Keychain toggles are active.
  • Authenticator Secret Keys: Open 2FA authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, VIP Access). Turn on cloud backup within the app settings or export your accounts via QR code to a secondary device. Resetting without an authenticator backup can permanently lock you out of Australian banking and government (myGov) portals.
  • Apple Pay & Wallet: When resetting via Settings, Apple automatically removes transit cards and credit cards from the Secure Element. If you are doing a hardware DFU restore, you can unbind cards remotely at appleid.apple.com.
  • eSIM Carrier Profiles: During the iOS reset dialog, you will be prompted: "Keep eSIMs and Erase Data" or "Erase All". If you intend to continue using your iPhone 16 Pro on your current Telstra, Optus, or Vodafone plan, always choose Keep eSIMs to avoid having to request a replacement QR code.

Anti-Theft Lock Avoidance Protocol (iCloud Activation Lock)

Every modern Apple device features iCloud Activation Lock, which is tied directly to Apple's T2/Secure Enclave security chip. If you reset your iPhone 16 Pro without signing out of your Apple ID first, the device will demand the original Apple ID email and password during setup before it can be used.

How to Safely Unlink Your Apple ID Before Resetting:

  1. Open Settings and tap on your name banner at the very top.
  2. Tap Find My → Find My iPhone and toggle it OFF. Enter your Apple ID password when prompted.
  3. Return to your Apple ID menu, scroll to the bottom, and tap Sign Out.
  4. Choose whether to keep a local copy of contacts and calendars, then confirm Sign Out. Once signed out, your iPhone 16 Pro can be safely restored without triggering Activation Lock.

Hardware Button Recovery & Deep Troubleshooting

If your iPhone 16 Pro has a frozen screen, bootloops on the Apple logo, or you have forgotten your lockscreen passcode, recovery mode is the definitive fallback:

  • Precision Key Timing: The Volume Up → Volume Down → Side button sequence must be executed rhythmically: quick-press and release Vol Up (1 second), quick-press and release Vol Down (1 second), then immediately press and hold the Side button without pausing.
  • Do Not Release at the Apple Logo: Many users release the Side button when they see the initial white Apple logo. Keep holding the Side button continuously for 15–20 seconds until the black Recovery screen appears (showing a computer and lightning/USB-C cable graphic).
  • Mac vs Windows Connection: On macOS Catalina and newer, open Finder and select your iPhone 16 Pro in the sidebar under Locations. On Windows or older macOS, launch iTunes or the official Apple Devices app. When prompted, select Restore (which wipes and installs fresh iOS) rather than Update.
  • DFU (Device Firmware Update) Mode: For deeply corrupted software, DFU mode allows firmware flashing without loading the bootloader. If Finder or iTunes fails in standard recovery, consult our technicians or visit a local repair shop for hardware-level flashing.

Post-Reset Setup & Performance Optimization

Once the wipe process finishes and your iPhone 16 Pro restarts to the initial setup screen ("Hello" / "Welcome"), follow these steps for a clean, stable experience:

  • Initial Setup Configuration: Connect to a secure Wi-Fi network and sign in with your primary account. If you plan to sell or donate your phone, stop at the language selection screen and power off the device so the recipient can configure their own profile.
  • Fresh Install vs Full Cloud Restore: If you performed a reset to fix sluggish performance, high system storage usage, or battery drain, consider setting up as a New Device and manually downloading essential apps rather than restoring an entire system snapshot. Restoring old system backups often re-imports cached app corruption and rogue background processes.
  • Install All System Updates: After setup, immediately check Settings → System → Software Update (or Settings → General → Software Update). Installing the latest security patches ensures all operating system libraries are refreshed and patched against known vulnerabilities.
  • Hardware Diagnostics: Verify that touch responsiveness across the entire digitizer, cameras, ear speaker, and charging ports are functioning properly. If your phone still experiences crashes, frozen screens, or overheating after a full factory reset, the root cause is almost certainly physical hardware damage (such as logic board failure, power IC damage, or degraded battery chemistry) that requires bench inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions — iPhone 16 Pro Reset

Will a factory reset erase the SIM card or eSIM on my iPhone 16 Pro?

No. A standard factory reset or recovery mode wipe only erases internal flash storage (user apps, photos, messages, and settings). Physical SIM cards are completely untouched. For eSIM profiles, iOS and Android explicitly prompt you whether to keep or remove eSIM plans during the reset process. Choose 'Keep eSIM' to retain your mobile plan.

Can I recover my photos and files after a hard reset on iPhone 16 Pro?

On modern smartphones featuring hardware-backed file-based encryption (FBE), performing a factory reset securely destroys the cryptographic master keys stored inside the Secure Enclave / Knox chipset. Without an existing cloud backup (iCloud, Google One, or PC backup), deleted files cannot be retrieved even by professional forensic data recovery tools.

Does a hard recovery reset remove Google FRP or iCloud Activation Lock?

No. Hardware recovery resets do NOT bypass Anti-Theft locks. Factory Reset Protection (FRP) and iCloud Activation Lock are stored in cloud security databases linked to the device hardware IMEI and serial number. The device will mandate entry of the original registered credentials upon booting up.

How do I reset my iPhone 16 Pro if the touchscreen is completely unresponsive?

If the screen is cracked or the digitizer does not respond to touch, use the Hardware Button Recovery method detailed in this guide. Because navigation in Recovery Mode relies exclusively on physical Volume and Power keys, you can initiate a full Wipe Data / Factory Reset without needing touch screen input.

What should I do if my iPhone 16 Pro is stuck in a bootloop after resetting?

If the device continually restarts or gets stuck on the brand logo after a wipe, force restart it and enter Recovery Mode again. Select 'Wipe cache partition' if available, or perform another 'Wipe data / factory reset'. If the issue persists, the operating system firmware may need to be re-flashed via official PC flashing software, or the motherboard/battery requires technician bench repair.
