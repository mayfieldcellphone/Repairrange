Don't just pick a plan by price. Understand the network tech, coverage tiers, and future satellite connectivity before you switch.

The "Three Tiers" of Australian Coverage

In Australia, there are only three physical phone networks: Telstra, Optus, and Vodafone. Every other provider (MVNO) "borrows" space on these networks. Here is how they compare:

Network Provider Coverage (Pop.) Best For The "Catch"
Telstra Retail 99.6% Rural, Remote, High-Speed 5G Most expensive plans in AU.
Boost Mobile 99.6% Rural Aussies on a Budget The only MVNO with Full Retail access.
Telstra Wholesale 98.8% Metro & Major Regional Missing ~1 million sq km of rural signal.
Optus Network 98.5% Value-seeking Metro users Network outages in 2023 caused trust issues.

Technical Tip

Antenna Signal Loss

Paying for a premium plan is useless if your hardware is failing. A cracked frame or loose internal antenna can drop your signal by 3-5 bars.

We see this every day at Mayfield Phone Repair. A simple chassis tune-up can restore "Outback-level" connectivity.
