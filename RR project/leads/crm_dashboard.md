## 🛠️ How to Update and Manage Your Local CRM

The local CRM runs directly on top of [outreach_schedule_june_2026.csv](outreach_schedule_june_2026.csv). 

1. **Automation updates:** Our Python script (`send_daily_batch.py`) will automatically transition leads from **`Pending`** to **`Sent`** and append the exact sending timestamp to the sheet.
2. **Manual updates:** When a shop owner replies and signs up for a trial, open the CSV file and manually update their `Status` column to **`Trial`** or **`Paid`**.
3. **Tracking Bounces:** If you receive a bounce email from Gmail, update the status for that specific shop to **`Bounced`** to exclude them from any follow-up sequence.
