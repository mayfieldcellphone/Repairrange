## 6. Competitor Awareness

### Direct Competitors (AU repair pricing)

- **WhatPhonePlan** — covers plans, not repairs. Not a direct competitor.
- **iFixit** — teardown guides + parts store. Global, not AU-focused. Our advantage: AU pricing, local city pages, bench expertise.
- **PhoneRepairCompare.com.au** — if it exists, likely thin content. Our advantage: editorial quality, real technician pricing.
- **Individual repair shop blogs** — fragmented, not comprehensive. Our advantage: one site covering all brands and cities.

### Our Differentiators

1. Built by a working repair technician (not a marketing company)
2. AU-specific pricing (not US data converted)
3. City-by-city pricing differentiation (Perth freight premium, tropical humidity in Cairns, etc.)
4. Honest about when NOT to repair (the 40% rule, repair vs replace guide)
5. Transparent about part quality tiers (OEM vs aftermarket vs budget)

## 8. CRM & Pipeline (HubSpot)

### Deal Pipeline Stages

1. **Lead** — contact scraped from Google Maps, not yet emailed
2. **Contacted** — outreach email sequence started
3. **Replied** — shop has responded (positive, negative, or question)
4. **Free Trial Active** — listing is live, free month running
5. **Listing Live** — shop has paid, listing is active
6. **Renewal Due** — listing period ending within 14 days
7. **Renewed** — shop has renewed for another period
8. **Churned** — shop chose not to renew (listing removed)

### Key Data to Track Per Contact

- Shop name
- Contact name + email
- City / city page
- Listing tier (Starter/Verified/Featured)
- Start date / end date
- Payment status
- Stripe payment link sent (Y/N)
- Notes (special requests, tagline, etc.)
