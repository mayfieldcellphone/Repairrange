Common Repair Questions

My screen doesn't respond to touch after installation

The display flex cable is likely not fully seated. Power off, reopen the phone, disconnect the screen cables, and reconnect them firmly — you should feel a gentle click. Also check that the battery connector is properly seated. If touch works only on part of the screen, the cable may be damaged.

Face ID stopped working after screen replacement

The Face ID module (earpiece/proximity sensor assembly) must be carefully transferred from your old screen to the new one. If it was damaged or not transferred, Face ID cannot be restored. If you transferred it but Face ID still fails, check the flex cable connection at the top of the screen — it may not be fully seated. See our screen repair guide for details.

Battery Health shows "Unknown" after battery replacement

This is normal for all non-Apple-installed batteries. Your battery works perfectly — iOS just can't verify the serial. If you have a diagnosable battery (included in SelfRepairKit kits), you can pair it using Apple's System Configuration tool to restore the Battery Health reading. See our non-genuine part warning guide.

Samsung fingerprint stopped working after screen replacement

If you installed an INCELL (Budget) screen on a Galaxy S-series phone, the ultrasonic fingerprint sensor is permanently disabled — it requires an OLED panel to function. You'll need to replace the screen with an OLED or OEM tier. See our INCELL vs OLED guide.

My screen has a yellow tint / True Tone is missing

True Tone requires the display calibration data to be transferred from the old screen or paired via Apple's System Configuration tool. INCELL screens do not support True Tone at all. OLED and OEM screens can have True Tone restored using a pairing tool or by visiting an Apple Authorised Service Provider ($20-$50 pairing fee).

Phone won't turn on after battery replacement

First, check the battery connector — it may not be fully seated. Reopen the phone and press the connector firmly until it clicks. If the phone still won't turn on, try a force restart (hold Power + Volume Down for 10 seconds). If nothing works, the battery may have arrived with zero charge — connect to a charger for 15 minutes, then try again.
