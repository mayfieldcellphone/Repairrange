iPhone Screen & Battery Replacement

Covers all iPhones from iPhone 7 to iPhone 17 Pro Max. Front-entry method.

iPhone Repair Guide

4-5 min · Screen & Battery · All Models

Click to play

Written Steps

1

Power off & remove bottom screws

Two pentalobe screws beside the charging port. Use the magnetic screwdriver from your kit. Place screws on the magnetic mat — they're tiny.

2

Heat edges & suction cup open

Hair dryer on low for 60-90 seconds around all edges. Attach suction cup near bottom, pull up gently, slide pry tool into gap. iPhone 7-SE3: opens left like a book. iPhone 12-17: opens from bottom upward.

Face ID warning: Don't insert pry tools more than 2mm on the top edge — the Face ID cable is right there.

3

Disconnect battery first

Remove the metal bracket (2-3 Phillips screws). Pop battery connector up with plastic spudger. This de-powers the phone — essential before touching any other cable.

4

Disconnect display cables

Remove the display cable bracket (3-4 tiny screws). Lift each connector straight up — don't slide or twist. There are typically 2-3 flex cables.

Screen repair: Transfer the earpiece speaker, front camera, and Face ID module from old screen to new. Battery repair: Skip this — just remove battery using pull tabs.

5

Install new part

Screen: Connect new screen cables (push until click). Battery: Apply new adhesive strips, lower battery into well, connect. Reconnect battery connector in both cases.

6

Seal & test everything

Apply adhesive strips around frame. Lower screen, press all edges. Replace bottom screws. Power on and test: touch response, Face ID, proximity sensor (make a call), front camera, True Tone, brightness.

Always test BEFORE final seal. If something doesn't work, it's much easier to re-open before the adhesive sets.

Model-Specific Notes

iPhone 17 / 17 Pro / Air

USB-C port. Titanium frame. Same front-entry process. Face ID cable on left side.

iPhone 15 / 16 series

USB-C port. Software battery pairing (health shows "Unknown" after DIY). Repair works fine.

iPhone 12 / 13 / 14 series

Lightning port. MagSafe magnet ring — don't displace during repair. Standard process.

iPhone 7 / 8 / SE / X / 11

Older models — cheapest kits, easiest repairs. No MagSafe. Lightning connector.

Samsung Galaxy Screen & Battery Replacement

Galaxy S21–S26 Ultra and A-series. Back-panel entry method.

Samsung Repair Guide

4-5 min · Screen & Battery · S & A Series

Click to play

Written Steps

1

Power off & heat back glass

Samsung opens from the BACK — not the front. Heat the back panel for 2-3 minutes with a hair dryer on high. Focus on edges where adhesive is strongest.

2

Pry off back glass

Suction cup on back, pull up, slide pry tool around all edges. Go slowly — back glass is thin and cracks easily. Re-heat if adhesive resists.

Water resistance: Opening the back breaks the IP68 seal. Re-seal with adhesive strips, but avoid submerging phone after.

3

Remove midframe & disconnect battery

Remove 8-12 Phillips screws holding the plastic midframe. Lift midframe off. Disconnect battery connector with plastic spudger.

4

Remove old part

Battery: Apply isopropyl alcohol (90%+) around edges, wait 60s, slide plastic card underneath. No pull tabs on Samsung — adhesive must be dissolved. Screen: Disconnect display cable, heat front screen edges, separate from frame.

Never pry battery with metal tools. Lithium cells can puncture and catch fire.

5

Install new part & reassemble

Place new part, connect cables, reconnect battery, replace midframe and all screws. Apply new back panel adhesive. Press back glass into position firmly around all edges.

6

Test everything

Power on. Test: touch response, fingerprint sensor (OLED/OEM only), charging, wireless charging, NFC, camera autofocus.

Samsung fingerprint: Budget INCELL screens don't support the ultrasonic sensor. Only OLED and OEM screens preserve fingerprint unlock.

Model-Specific Notes

Galaxy S24–S26 Ultra

Titanium frame (S24U+). Strong adhesive. Fingerprint sensor requires OLED or OEM screen.

Galaxy S21–S23 series

Standard aluminium frame. Slightly easier adhesive. Same fingerprint requirement.

Galaxy A55 / A54 / A35 / A25 / A15

Easiest Samsung repairs. Weaker adhesive. A15 can use INCELL (no fingerprint in screen).

Z Fold / Z Flip — DO NOT DIY

Folding mechanism too complex. Take to a professional.

OPPO Screen & Battery Replacement

Find, Reno, and A-series. Back-panel entry — similar to Samsung.

OPPO Repair Guide

3-4 min · Screen & Battery · All Models

Click to play

Written Steps

1

Power off & heat back panel

OPPO uses the same back-entry method as Samsung. Heat the back for 2-3 minutes. OPPO adhesive is generally weaker than Samsung — slightly easier to open.

2

Remove back panel & midframe

Suction cup, pry around edges, lift back off. Remove midframe screws (Phillips). OPPO typically uses 6-10 screws depending on model.

3

Disconnect battery & remove part

Pop battery connector. For battery: dissolve adhesive with isopropyl, slide plastic card under. For screen: disconnect display cable from board, heat front edges, separate screen from frame.

4

Install, reassemble & test

Place new part, reconnect cables, reassemble midframe, re-seal back panel. Test: touch, fingerprint (if applicable), charging, camera.

Model Notes

Find X8 / X7 / X6 / X5

Flagship. Curved screens on some models — more adhesive, more care needed.

Reno 10-12 series

Mid-range. Flat screens. Standard difficulty. Most popular OPPO repair.

A57 / A77 / A78 / A96 / A98

Budget. Easiest OPPO repairs. INCELL screens available. Cheapest kits.
