Can You Fix a Phone Screen Without Replacing It?

By SRK Engineering Team • June 21, 2026

It starts with a small drop. Then, a single hairline crack across your beautiful $1,500 display. The first instinct for many Australians is to search for a way to fix a phone screen without replacing it. But can it actually be done?

The Myths: Toothpaste, Baking Soda, and Magic Liquids

You may have seen "hacks" on social media suggesting that toothpaste or a paste made of baking soda can buff out a crack. As engineers, we can tell you plainly: this does not work. Modern smartphone screens are made of chemically strengthened glass. These household items might smooth out microscopic surface scratches, but they cannot repair a structural crack.

💡 Pro Tip:

Using liquid glass "gap fillers" often seeps into the digitizer layer, causing permanent touch-unresponsiveness. Don't risk your phone's motherboard for a $10 temporary fix.

The Reality: Why Replacement is the Only Safe Path

Once the glass is cracked, the structural integrity of the display is gone. Moisture, dust, and pressure from your thumb will eventually damage the OLED or LCD panel beneath. The most cost-effective and permanent solution is a full screen replacement using an engineer-approved kit.
