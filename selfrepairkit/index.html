How it Works: 3 Steps to a Flawless DIY Screen Fix

01

Soften the Adhesive Seal

Use a household hair dryer or a heat pad around the perimeter of your iPhone bezel for roughly 60 seconds to safely soften the underlying water-resistance adhesive.

02

Apply the Suction Cup & Lift

Place our heavy-duty suction cup just above the Home button or lower screen edge. Pull up gently while sliding the ultra-thin opening pick into the seam to pop the clips.

03

Track Your Screws

As you remove the internal silver shield plates, place each screw on the corresponding node on your included Magnetic Project Mat. Swap your screen, reconnect the cables, and button it back up.
