Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026 · SelfRepairKit Australia · ABN pending

14-Day Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for unused, unopened items in their original packaging. If you have changed your mind or ordered the wrong model, you may return the item for a refund or exchange.

To be eligible for a return, the item must be unused, in the same condition you received it, and in the original packaging with all included tools and accessories.

Exchanges

We accept exchanges within 14 days of delivery. If you ordered the wrong model, screen type, or quality tier, contact us and we will arrange an exchange for the correct kit. You are responsible for return shipping costs. We will dispatch the replacement kit once we receive and inspect the returned item.

Faulty or Damaged Items — 14-Day Replacement

If your item arrives broken, faulty, or damaged, contact us within 14 days of delivery. We will replace the faulty item at no cost to you, including return shipping. Please provide photos of the damage or fault when contacting us.

This is in addition to our 90-day warranty that covers manufacturing defects on all screen and battery kits.

Refund Process

Once your returned item is received and inspected, we will notify you via email. If approved, your refund will be processed within 7 business days to your original payment method (PayID, PayPal, or bank transfer).

Please note that it may take an additional 3–5 business days for your bank or payment provider to reflect the refund in your account.

Return Shipping

Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item is faulty or damaged on arrival. We recommend using a tracked shipping service — we are not responsible for items lost in return transit.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

Items that have been installed, used, or show signs of attempted installation. Adhesive strips, screen protectors, and consumable accessories once opened. Items returned after 14 days from delivery without prior arrangement.

Australian Consumer Law

Our returns policy operates alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights as an Australian consumer, including your right to a repair, replacement, or refund for products with a major fault.

How to Request a Return
