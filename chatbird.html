ChatBird

One inbox for your customer messages and reviews

ChatBird is a shared inbox for small businesses. It brings customer conversations and Google reviews into a single place, so a small team can see what needs a reply and respond quickly.

What ChatBird does

Shared inbox. Messages from connected channels such as Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and SMS appear in one list.
Google Business Profile reviews. When the business owner connects their Google account, ChatBird shows the business's Google reviews, lets the team reply to them, alerts the team about new reviews, and helps send customers the business's review link.
Owner-controlled. Every connection is made by the business owner, who can disconnect it at any time from the Integrations page.

Who runs ChatBird

ChatBird is operated by Mayfield Phone Repair, 276 Maitland Rd, Mayfield NSW 2304, Australia, for its own businesses and for the businesses it invites to use the inbox.

Google account access

ChatBird asks for access to a Google Business Profile only to read and respond to that business's reviews. Read how we handle Google user data in our Privacy Policy.
