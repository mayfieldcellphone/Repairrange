GTA VI Pricing: What Aussie Gamers Can Expect

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 25, 2026

Introduction

After much speculation and excitement, Rockstar Games has finally announced pre-orders for the highly-anticipated GTA VI, starting from June 25. One of the key mysteries surrounding this release has been its pricing. While the publisher assured fans that it wouldn't be outrageous, it still left us all wondering: what does that really mean?

Official Pricing Announced

Well, wonder no more! The official pricing has been revealed: the Standard Edition of GTA VI will set you back $80 AUD. If you're keen on the Deluxe Edition, you'll need to fork out $100 AUD. This pricing structure aims to make the game accessible to a broader audience, without compromising on quality.

What Do These Prices Mean for Aussie Gamers?

While $80 for a new game may not seem too steep, it's essential to consider what’s included in this pricing. Rockstar is known for delivering expansive, content-rich experiences, and GTA VI is expected to be no exception. With a promise of enhanced graphics, gameplay mechanics, and an engaging storyline, it seems like a worthy investment for any gaming aficionado.

FAQs About GTA VI Pre-Orders

  • When can I pre-order GTA VI? Pre-orders officially open on June 25.
  • Will there be any special editions? Yes, aside from the Standard Edition, you can also opt for the Deluxe Edition for extra features.
