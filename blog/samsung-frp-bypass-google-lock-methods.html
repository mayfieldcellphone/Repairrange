How to Bypass Google Lock (FRP) on Samsung Devices in 2026

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing · Updated 15 August 2026

Published on June 15, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

If you have recently factory reset your Samsung Galaxy device, you may have run headfirst into a screen reading: "Verify your account. This device was reset. To continue, sign in with a Google Account that was previously synced on this device."

This security mechanism is called FRP (Factory Reset Protection). While it prevents thieves from using stolen phones, it is a massive headache for legitimate owners who bought secondhand devices or forgot old Gmail credentials.

Method 1: The Emergency Dialer "No-PC" Bypass Code (*#0*#)

  1. Power on your locked device and return to the Welcome screen.
  2. Tap on Emergency Call.
  3. Dial: *#0*#
  4. If the hardware diagnostic screen appears, connect the phone to your PC.
  5. Open a utility like SamFw Tool and click Remove FRP.
  6. Accept the "Allow USB Debugging" prompt on the phone.

Method 2: SamFw Tool (PC Required)

For newer security patches, you must use a PC-based program to communicate with the phone’s bootloader.

  • Install Samsung USB Mobile Drivers on Windows.
  • Download the official SamFw Tool.
  • Connect your device and select the Samsung tab.
  • Click Remove FRP [New Method] and wait for the restart.
