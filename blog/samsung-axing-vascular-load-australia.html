Samsung Axes Vascular Load Feature in Aussie Smartwatches

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 04, 2026

Samsung’s Vascular Load Feature Gets the Chop

In a surprising move, Samsung has announced the removal of its Vascular Load feature from its smartwatches across the US, and it seems this change is also making waves Down Under. With the rollout of Samsung Health version 7.0 and One UI 9 Watch slated for late July, Aussie users of the Galaxy Watch series should prepare for a transition.

What Does This Mean for Aussie Users?

The Vascular Load feature, which has been popular among health enthusiasts, will no longer be part of the Samsung smartwatch experience. The transition occurs as Samsung prepares to unveil its latest models, the Galaxy Watch9 and Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, rumoured to debut on July 22. These new devices will come equipped with One UI 9 Watch, but without the Vascular Load capability.

A Look at What’s Coming

While the removal of Vascular Load might disappoint some users, the Galaxy Watch9 and Ultra 2 are expected to bring other exciting features that promote health and wellness tracking. As always, staying updated with Samsung's latest offerings will help users determine if these new watches meet their fitness and lifestyle needs.
