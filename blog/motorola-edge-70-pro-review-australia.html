Motorola Edge 70 Pro Review: A Look at the Latest Flagship

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 21, 2026

Introducing the Motorola Edge 70 Pro

The Motorola Edge 70 Pro has finally landed on Australian shores, offering a wide array of features geared towards tech enthusiasts. Launched in April, this device boasts a competitive price tag of around €540, or approximately AUD 860, based on current exchange rates. The flagship features 8GB of RAM and a generous 256GB of internal storage, making it an appealing option for those who require performance and space.

Vibrant Colour Palette

One of the standout features of the Edge 70 Pro is its variety of Pantone colours available for Aussie customers. Whether you prefer the bold Titan, the rich Zinfandel, classic Lily White, or the tranquil Tea, there’s a colour to fit your unique style. This attention to aesthetic will certainly appeal to those looking for a device that looks as good as it performs.

What’s in the Box?

Upon unboxing your new Motorola Edge 70 Pro, you'll find the phone itself, a high-quality USB-C charging cable, and a hard case for protection against accidental drops. This comprehensive package ensures you’re ready to roll as soon as you open the box.

Is the Motorola Edge 70 Pro Right for You?

With impressive specifications and design, the Motorola Edge 70 Pro is undoubtedly a strong contender in the current smartphone market. If you're in the hunt for a reliable device that balances power and style, this might just be the perfect fit for you.
