Galaxy Crew Expands: Samsung's One UI 9 Testing Reaches More Aussie Favourites!

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing

Published on June 10, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

The Next Big Refresh: One UI 9 is Gearing Up!

Here at RepairRange.io, we're always keeping our finger on the pulse of what's happening in the Aussie tech landscape, not just for repair costs, but for the crucial software updates that keep our beloved devices humming. There's a buzz in the air, and it's all about One UI 9, Samsung's next major software overlay built on the foundation of Android 17. While the shiny new Galaxy S26 series already has a taste of the beta, and we knew the Galaxy S25 range and the anticipated S26 FE were on the list, Samsung is now widening its net significantly.

This is fantastic news for a broader spectrum of Aussie Galaxy owners, as it signals that the latest enhancements, security patches, and user experience refinements are making their way to more devices sooner rather than later. It's not just about the flagship experience anymore; Samsung is clearly committed to bringing its cutting-edge software to a wider audience, from the premium to the ever-popular mid-range.

Mid-Range Marvels Join the One UI 9 Party

The latest intel confirms that three more popular Samsung Galaxy smartphones are now officially undergoing testing for the One UI 9 update. This is a significant move, as it includes devices that are often everyday workhorses for many Australians. So, which devices are getting ready for their big software glow-up?

  • Galaxy A17 5G: A popular choice for those seeking 5G connectivity without breaking the bank, the A17 5G is set to get a fresh lease on life.
  • Galaxy A34: Another strong contender in the mid-range segment, the A34's inclusion means its loyal user base can look forward to the latest features.
  • Galaxy A57: For many, the A57 offers an excellent balance of features and affordability, and it's great to see it in the One UI 9 testing pipeline.

These additions highlight Samsung's strategy to deliver a consistent, up-to-date software experience across its diverse portfolio. For owners of these devices, this means improved performance, new customisation options, and potentially better battery life are on the horizon. The faster these updates are tested, the quicker they can roll out to your device, ensuring you're running the most secure and feature-rich version of Android.

What Does Android 17 & One UI 9 Mean for Your Galaxy?

While specific features for One UI 9 are usually revealed closer to its public release, we can generally expect a suite of improvements that build upon Google's Android 17 foundation. Historically, new Android versions bring enhancements in areas like:

  • Privacy & Security: More granular controls over data access, app permissions, and enhanced security features to keep your information safe.
  • Customisation: Deeper options for personalising your phone's look and feel, from lock screen widgets to dynamic theming based on your wallpaper.
  • Performance Optimisation: Better resource management, leading to smoother animations, faster app launches, and potentially improved battery endurance.
  • New Features: Google and Samsung always surprise us with innovative functionalities that enhance daily usage, whether it's smarter notifications or more intuitive navigation.

For many Australians who hold onto their phones for a few years, these updates are crucial. They not only provide new functionalities but also extend the practical lifespan of the device by keeping it secure and relevant in an ever-evolving digital world.
