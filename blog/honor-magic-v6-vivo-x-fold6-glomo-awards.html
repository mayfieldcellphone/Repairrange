Honor Magic V6 and vivo X Fold6 Shine at GLOMO Asia Awards

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 27, 2026

Highlights from Mobile World Congress Shanghai

The highly anticipated Mobile World Congress (MWC) Shanghai is currently underway from June 24 to June 26, showcasing the latest advancements in mobile technology. Among the significant highlights of the event are the GLOMO Asia Awards, which celebrate exceptional innovations in the mobile sector.

Honor Magic V6: A Winning Smartphone

Leading the pack this year is the Honor Magic V6, which has impressively claimed two awards. It was recognised as the Best Smartphone and also took home the award for Disruptive Device Innovation. These awards reflect the device's advanced features, sleek design, and its user-friendly experience that stands out in a competitive market.

vivo X Fold6: Acknowledged for Excellence

Not far behind, the vivo X Fold6 received the prestigious title of Best In Show - Product. This foldable smartphone has captured attention with its innovative design and functionality, making it a strong contender in the foldable segment. Its ability to combine power and versatility is setting a new gold standard for foldable devices.
