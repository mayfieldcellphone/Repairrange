Introducing Pocket: The AI Note-Taking Solution

In an age where technology continues to evolve, Pocket has stepped up to the plate with an exciting new product tailored for the Australian market. With a recent funding boost of $11 million, Pocket aims to meet the surging demand for efficient and innovative note-taking devices.

What is Pocket?

Pocket is a sleek, credit card-sized device priced at $129. It can easily stick to the back of your smartphone, blending seamlessly into your daily life. But don’t let its size fool you; this compact puck is packed with powerful capabilities.

Features That Make a Difference

This gadget is designed to offer you unlimited recordings, transcriptions, and to-do lists. Whether you're in a lecture, meeting, or just brainstorming ideas, Pocket promises to keep all your notes organised and accessible.

Why Aussies Will Love It

With the rise in remote work and online learning, Australians are constantly looking for tools that enhance productivity. Pocket's ease of use and versatile functionality makes it an attractive option for students, professionals, and anyone needing to streamline their note-taking process.

How Pocket fits the wider AI hardware wave

Pocket fits into the wider AI-hardware wave — dedicated assistants, AI e-ink notebooks and wearable recorders are all landing in 2026 with the same promise: offload your notes from the phone. They also share the same repair profile as any new gadget: a screen, battery and charging port that will eventually need service. Replacement parts for first-gen AI devices are scarce at launch, so a working unit is worth protecting — screen protector, case, and a sensible charging habit extend the life of a device you can't yet get OEM parts for. If it breaks, a local repair bench can usually handle the battery and port before you consider replacing the whole unit.
