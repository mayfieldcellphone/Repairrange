Meet the iQOO Z11i

Exciting news for tech enthusiasts as the iQOO Z11i makes its debut in Australia! One of the standout features of the iQOO Z11i is its massive 6,500mAh battery. Say goodbye to frequent charging breaks; this smartphone can keep you connected throughout the day, making it perfect for the Aussie lifestyle where outdoor adventures and long commutes are the norm.

Stunning Display

The iQOO Z11i boasts a 6.74-inch LCD display, providing an immersive visual experience. Sporting an impressive refresh rate of 120Hz and HD+ resolution, gaming and video playback will be smooth as silk. Whether you’re streaming your favourite show or battling it out in the latest games, the Z11i ensures every detail is crystal clear.

With the integration of the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 processor, you can expect seamless multitasking and quick app launches. Coupled with the sizeable battery, it’s an ideal choice for those who rely heavily on their smartphones for both work and play.
