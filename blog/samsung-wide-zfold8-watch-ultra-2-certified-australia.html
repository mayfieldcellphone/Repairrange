Aussie Alert: Samsung's Wide Galaxy Z Fold8 & Watch Ultra 2 Get Green Light for Launch!

Published on June 11, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

Kicking Off the Buzz Down Under: Samsung's New Toys Get Their Green Light

G'day, tech enthusiasts! Here at RepairRange.io, we're always keeping an ear to the ground for the latest whispers and official nods in the mobile tech world. And this week, Samsung has given us a couple of juicy tidbits to chew on. Their much-anticipated, wider-format Galaxy Z Fold8 and the beefed-up Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 have just cleared significant regulatory hurdles, signalling they’re officially one step closer to landing in our eager Aussie hands.

Certifications might sound a bit dry, but trust us, they’re the critical bureaucratic hoops every major device has to jump through before hitting the market. For us Aussies, it means these premium gadgets are gearing up for their global debut, and our local launch won’t be far behind!

The Wide World of the Galaxy Z Fold8: A New Horizon for Foldables?

Let's dive into the foldable that's creating a stir: the new wide-format Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8. Fresh off the press, this device, identified by the model number SM-F971B, has reportedly received its BIS certification in India. While this specific certification is for the Indian market, it’s a strong indicator that global availability, including for us Down Under, is imminent.

The "wide-format" aspect is particularly intriguing. For years, one common feedback point for foldables has been the narrow external display. A wider form factor could drastically improve the usability of the outer screen, making it feel less like a remote control and more like a traditional smartphone when folded. Imagine less scrolling, more comfortable typing, and a generally more satisfying experience before you even unfold it into that gorgeous tablet-sized display!

There's been some chatter about Samsung's naming strategy – with speculation suggesting this 'wide' model might simply be the main Z Fold8, while a more conventional Z Fold7 successor could be dubbed a Z Fold8 Ultra. Regardless of the branding gymnastics, the core takeaway is exciting: Samsung is innovating on the physical dimensions of its flagship foldable, aiming to enhance the everyday user experience significantly. And for repair specialists like us, a new design always brings new challenges and opportunities for keeping these cutting-edge devices in top shape.

Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: Time for an Upgrade on Your Wrist?

Not to be overshadowed, the robust Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 has also been spotted making its way through the certification process. While details are scant at this stage – the certification itself doesn't spill the beans on specs or new features – its very existence confirms Samsung is set to refresh its top-tier smartwatch line.

The original Galaxy Watch Ultra carved out a niche for itself with its durability, extended battery life, and advanced fitness tracking capabilities. We can expect the Ultra 2 to build on these strengths, perhaps with improved health sensors, even more rugged materials, or enhanced outdoor navigation features. For those of us who push our tech to its limits, whether it’s hitting the surf, conquering hiking trails, or just enduring the daily grind, a new 'Ultra' watch from Samsung is always something to look forward to.
