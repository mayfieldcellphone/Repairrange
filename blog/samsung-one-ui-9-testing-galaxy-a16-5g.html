A Comprehensive Guide to Samsung's One UI 9 Testing on the Galaxy A16 5G

Published on June 16, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

Understanding the Transition to One UI 9

Last month, Samsung rolled out the stable One UI 8.5 update for the Galaxy A16 5G, enhancing the smartphone's performance and user experience. Now, Samsung has taken the next step by initiating tests for One UI 9, pushing the boundaries of what's possible with this device.

What Does One UI 9 Offer?

The upcoming One UI 9, based on Android 17, is set to introduce a plethora of new features and improvements aimed at optimising user interaction. With the firmware version A166BXXU8EZF1 already noted on Samsung's server, what can users expect from this update?

  • Enhanced User Interface: One UI 9 promises a more intuitive and seamless experience, focussing on ease of navigation.
  • Performance Improvements: Optimisations for speed and efficiency to ensure the Galaxy A16 5G runs smoothly under heavier loads.
  • New Features: Innovative tools that leverage the power of Android 17.

How to Check for Updates

For Aussies eager to try out One UI 9, checking for updates is straightforward:

  1. Open Settings on your Galaxy A16 5G.
  2. Scroll down and tap on Software Update.
  3. Select Download and Install.

✅ Good to know: Keep your device connected to Wi-Fi and ensure it has adequate battery life to avoid interruptions during the update process.
