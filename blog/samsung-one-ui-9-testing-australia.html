Samsung's One UI 9 Testing Expands for Aussie Galaxy Users

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 04, 2026

Welcome to the Next Generation of One UI

Samsung has officially kicked off its testing for the upcoming One UI 9, based on Android 17. While we just welcomed the stable One UI 8.5 for the Galaxy A26 back in May, anticipation is building for what's to come. For those in Australia waiting for the latest features, this news is particularly exciting!

What’s New in One UI 9?

Although there isn’t a full list of features yet, early reports suggest that One UI 9 will focus on enhanced usability, new visual elements, and improved performance. Users can expect a more intuitive experience, making everyday tasks smoother and more enjoyable.

Devices in the Testing Phase

Samsung is not just working on the Galaxy A26; a variety of other Galaxy models are also in line to receive the One UI 9 update. This broadening of the testing base means that many Aussies will soon be able to experience the fresh look and feel of this latest UI.

When Can You Expect One UI 9?

While there’s no confirmed date for the official rollout just yet, past patterns indicate that users can expect One UI 9 to debut in the coming months. Stay tuned to your software updates, as more details will become available leading up to the launch.
