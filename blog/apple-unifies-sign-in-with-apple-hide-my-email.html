Streamlining Privacy: Apple Unifies Sign in With Apple and Hide My Email

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing · Updated 15 August 2026

Published on June 16, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

Understanding the New Email Domain for Apples Services

In an effort to bolster user privacy, Apple has announced a significant change that will unify the email domains used for its Sign in with Apple and iCloud+ Hide My Email features. This initiative aims to simplify the management of email addresses and enhance privacy for users across Australia and beyond.

What This Change Means for Users

Currently, when you use Sign in with Apple, the service generates randomly assigned email addresses that utilize the privaterelay.appleid.com domain. Meanwhile, the Hide My Email function under iCloud+ operates using a different set of email addresses. With the upcoming update, both services will converge under a singular private.icloud.com domain.

✅ Good to know: This transition is expected to enhance the user experience by making your emails more manageable and recognizable while still keeping user information private.

Benefits of Using the Unified Domain

  • Enhanced Privacy: Both features will offer better privacy assurances as they rely on Apple's trusted infrastructure.
  • Simplified Management: Users will find it easier to keep track of their generated email addresses as they will all fall under a common domain.
  • Improved Security: The unification aims at reducing potential points of failure and increasing security for personal information.

Next Steps and Considerations

As this change rolls out, users should consider reviewing their settings and understanding how their email addresses are generated and used. Keeping your email communication secure is paramount, and this transition makes it easier.

⚠️ Warning: Always ensure that you are using trusted updates and email features directly from Apple, avoiding any sketchy third-party applications that promise similar functionalities.
