Bookshop.org's Kobo eReader Support Set to Launch in 2023

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 07, 2026

Introduction

In a significant turn of events, Bookshop.org, a rising competitor to Amazon in the book retail landscape, has confirmed that Kobo eReader support is finally set to launch in 2023. After some uncertainty surrounding their partnership, the company has announced to TechCrunch that they have ironed out the necessary business terms and are currently developing the integration. This news is particularly exciting for Australian readers who are looking for more versatile options in digital reading.

What This Means for Aussie Readers

Australians have long enjoyed a vibrant ebook culture, and the addition of Bookshop.org's support for Kobo eReaders promises to enhance their reading experience. With its emphasis on independent bookshops and community support, Bookshop.org has carved out a unique niche. This integration allows readers to enjoy their favourite titles while supporting local businesses – a win-win situation for the literary community.

Improved Features on the Horizon

The collaboration aims to seamlessly integrate Bookshop.org’s vast inventory with Kobo’s user-friendly interface. This means that as a reader, you won’t just have access to a wealth of titles, but you can also enjoy the convenience of Kobo's robust reading features. Imagine obtaining your favourite new releases from local bookstores directly to your eReader. It’s an exciting prospect!

The Future of Ebook Retailing

For those of us who believe in the power of independent bookstores, this partnership represents an important shift in the ebook retail landscape. Bookshop.org's mission aligns closely with fostering local communities, which we certainly appreciate here in Australia. Keep an eye out for more updates as the integration progresses!
