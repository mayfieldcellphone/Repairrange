Exciting News for Aussie Samsung Fans: One UI 9 Testing Begins!

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 21, 2026

One UI 9 Testing for Samsung Devices

Samsung is kicking off its testing phase for One UI 9, and the excitement is palpable among Aussie tech enthusiasts. This new update promises a host of features and improvements that can elevate your smartphone experience.

Devices in the Testing Spotlight

Currently, Samsung has released the third beta of One UI 9, with the testing phase extending to several models:

  • Galaxy S23 series
  • Galaxy Z Fold7
  • Galaxy A56
  • Galaxy S24 series

These devices are at the forefront of Samsung’s innovation and will benefit greatly from the enhancements that One UI 9 aims to introduce.

What’s New in One UI 9?

While detailed features haven't been fully unveiled yet, early indications suggest significant improvements in performance, user interface design, and additional functionalities that will make your interactions smoother than ever. Samsung has been listening closely to user feedback, and it appears that One UI 9 will indeed cater to many of these requests.

How to Participate in the One UI 9 Beta

If you're eager to test out One UI 9 on your Galaxy device, keep an eye out for beta sign-ups. As of now, the beta program is exclusive to the Galaxy S26 series, but Samsung is reportedly expanding it to other models like the Galaxy S25 series and Galaxy A series soon.
