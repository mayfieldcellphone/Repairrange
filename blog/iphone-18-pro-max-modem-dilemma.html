RR

The Modem Breakdown for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max

Exciting news is on the horizon for Apple enthusiasts down under, with the anticipation growing for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. However, recent leaks have unveiled a rather complicated modem strategy that Apple is employing in these new models, particularly relevant for Australians looking to upgrade.

What We Know So Far

Recent schematics obtained from a cyberattack on Apple’s supplier Tata have revealed that the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max will utilise two different types of modems. In a strategic move, Apple has opted to stick with Qualcomm for the US market, leaving many to wonder about the implications for Australian users.

Why the Choice of Modems Matters

The decision to incorporate both a Qualcomm modem and a proprietary Apple modem highlights the company's efforts to tailor its offerings based on regional performance requirements. This might be linked to the unique network specifications and demands in each country, including Australia.

What Does This Mean for Australian Users?

For those of us in Australia, the switch may not make a visible difference initially, but it’s essential to keep an eye on how network performance varies based on the modem used. Apple’s proprietary modem could yield benefits in terms of battery life and efficiency, but it’s unclear how it will stack up against Qualcomm's tried-and-tested technology.
