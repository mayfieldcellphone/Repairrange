Unleashing Bilingual Captions on Instagram Edits: A Guide for Aussies

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 03, 2026

What’s New with Instagram Edits?

Since its launch last year, Instagram has been expanding its Edits app to compete with platforms like CapCut. Recently, the app has rolled out some exciting new features that cater specifically to the needs of content creators. Among these updates is the addition of bilingual captions, allowing you to reach a broader audience.

Bilingual Captions: Making Your Content Accessible

The new bilingual captions feature in Instagram Edits is a game-changer. With this tool, Instagram automatically translates your captions into a secondary language. Currently, supported languages include English, Indonesian, Russian, and Portuguese—perfect for engaging with diverse audiences in Australia and beyond.

How to Use Bilingual Captions

To enable bilingual captions, simply navigate to the settings in your Edits app and choose your preferred secondary language. Once set, every caption you create will automatically translate, making your posts more inclusive. Whether you're posting about the stunning landscapes of Australia or your latest DIY tips, this feature helps everyone feel connected.

New Templates to Spark Creativity

Alongside the bilingual functionality, Instagram Edits has introduced a range of new templates. These fresh designs offer a variety of visual options, making it even easier to create stunning videos that catch the eye. Try out different templates to elevate your storytelling and share your adventures, tips, or creative projects effortlessly.
