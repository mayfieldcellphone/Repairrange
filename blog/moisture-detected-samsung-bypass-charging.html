How to Bypass Samsung 'Moisture Detected' Charging Error

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing

Published on June 16, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

You plug your Samsung Galaxy device into its charger, and instead of power flowing, your screen lights up with a red warning: "Moisture detected. To charge your device, make sure your charger/USB port is dry."

This safety protocol is built into flagships (from Galaxy S8 to S24) to prevent galvanic corrosion. However, the sensor is prone to misfires caused by pocket lint, sea air, or software glitches. Here is the safest way to diagnose and bypass the error.

Method 1: Safe Physical Drying and Cleaning

  1. Do Not Use Heat: High temperatures melt gaskets and adhesives.
  2. Avoid Raw Rice: Rice dust enters the port and forms a concrete-like paste.
  3. The Cold Air Trick: Place the phone in front of a fan or cold hair dryer for 15 minutes.
  4. Lint Extraction: Use a wooden toothpick to gently sweep debris out of the port.

Method 2: Soft-Reset Boot Bypass

You can bypass the sensor check during the boot sequence as the detection daemon loads late.

  1. Power off the device.
  2. Plug the cable into the phone.
  3. Immediately hold Power + Volume Down for 15 seconds to force a reboot.
  4. The device will boot past the initial check and continue charging.

Method 3: Clearing the 'USBSettings' Cache

  1. Go to Settings > Apps.
  2. Enable "Show system apps" in the filter.
  3. Find USBSettings.
  4. Tap Storage > Clear Data.
