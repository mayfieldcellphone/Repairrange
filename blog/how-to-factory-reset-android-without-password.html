How to Factory Reset Any Android Phone Without the Password

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing · Updated 15 August 2026

Published on July 3, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

We've all been there: you forget your lock screen PIN, or you've found an old device and can't remember the pattern. If you need to wipe the device to start fresh, a Hardware Factory Reset is your best option.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: READ THIS FIRST

Performing a hardware reset will delete all data on the device. Additionally, modern Android phones feature FRP (Factory Reset Protection). After the reset, you MUST log in with the original Google Account. If you don't know it, you will need our FRP Bypass Guide.

The Master Method: Recovery Mode

Because you cannot access the settings menu, you must use physical button combinations to enter the phone's hidden Recovery Mode.

1. Samsung Galaxy Devices

For most modern Samsung phones (S21, S22, S23, S24):

  1. Turn off the device.
  2. Plug the phone into a PC via USB cable (Modern Samsung models require this to enter recovery).
  3. Press and hold Power + Volume Up until the Samsung logo appears.
  4. Release buttons when the Recovery Menu (blue/black screen) appears.

2. Oppo & Realme Devices

  1. Turn off the device.
  2. Press and hold Power + Volume Down.
  3. Release when the Oppo logo appears. Select 'English' to enter the main menu.

3. Motorola & Google Pixel Devices

  1. Turn off the device.
  2. Press and hold Power + Volume Down.
  3. Use volume keys to highlight Recovery Mode and press Power to select.

Wiping the Data

Once you are in the Recovery Menu:

  • Use the Volume Keys to scroll down to 'Wipe data/factory reset'.
  • Press the Power Button to select it.
  • Confirm by selecting 'Factory data reset'.
  • Once finished, select 'Reboot system now'.
