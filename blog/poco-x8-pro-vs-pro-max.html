Poco X8 Pro vs Pro Max: Which One's Worth Your AUD?

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing

Published on June 18, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

The Battle of the Poco Giants

As Xiaomi continues to make waves in the Aussie tech scene, the Poco X8 series has sparked a lot of interest. Both the Poco X8 Pro and the Poco X8 Pro Max are vying for your attention, but is the extra investment in the Pro Max justified? Let’s dive into the main features of these two models.

What’s Different? A Closer Look

When it comes to choosing between the Poco X8 Pro and the Pro Max, it’s important to understand what sets them apart:

  • Price Disparity: The gap is around €100-120 in Europe or approximately AUD 180-240, making it a significant decision based on your budget.
  • Camera Capabilities: The Pro Max boasts upgrades in its camera setup, promising better low-light performance and enhanced image processing.
  • Battery Specifications: While both models offer ample battery life, the Pro Max’s battery management features can provide longer usage times under similar conditions.

Design and User Experience

Both models come packed with sleek designs that cater to modern aesthetics while also ensuring durability. The Poco X8 Pro offers a solid grip and lightweight feel, but the Pro Max takes it a notch higher with its premium finish and enhanced ergonomics. Users can expect:

  • Screen Quality: The display on both phones is vibrant and colourful, but the Pro Max features better brightness levels for outdoor visibility.
  • User Interface: Both devices run on MIUI, but the Pro Max benefits from firmware optimisations that might slightly improve overall performance.

Why Tracking Repair Costs Matters

Owning a smartphone is an investment, and knowing how to manage it means keeping tabs on potential repair costs. With the Poco X8 series, users are encouraged to check out RepairRange.io. Here’s why:

  • Accurate Pricing: Understanding screen and battery repair costs can save you from overpaying at local repair shops.
  • Learning Trends: Stay updated on common issues associated with your device model to anticipate any future repairs.

Being informed plays a crucial role in protecting your investment and ensuring that you maintain the best possible use of your Poco X8 device.
