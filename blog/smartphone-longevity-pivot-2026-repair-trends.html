The Longevity Pivot: Why Australians are Keeping Phones for 5+ Years in 2026

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing · Updated 15 August 2026

Smartphone Average Selling Prices (ASPs) have hit a record high of approximately $700 AUD in 2026. This surge is driving a "Longevity Pivot" among consumers.

In 2026, the tech market looks very different than it did just a few years ago. Global supply chain shifts and the premiumization of devices like the iPhone 17 Pro and Galaxy S26 have pushed prices into a new tier. In response, a staggering 28% of Australian consumers are now choosing to keep their devices for 5 years or more.

The New "Repair vs Replace" Math

When a new flagship phone costs over $2,000, the $250 repair bill for a screen or $99 for a battery refresh suddenly feels like a bargain. The "repair vs replace" math heavily favors keeping your current device alive. We're seeing a massive increase in demand for "mid-life maintenance" services.

Instead of waiting for a catastrophic failure, owners are opting for preventive care to ensure their high-value investment lasts as long as possible.

Why It's Smart for Your Wallet

By investing in a $150 maintenance service every 2-3 years, you can easily double the lifespan of your device. This avoids the "upgrade cycle" that keeps many consumers in permanent debt or paying high monthly contract fees. In 2026, the smartest tech move isn't buying the newest phone—it's taking care of the one you already have.
