Windows Laptop Repair Cost in Australia (2026): Screens, Batteries & Hinges

Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS and Acer repair pricing, job by job — plus the diagnostic fees that don't appear on any price list, and the point where repairing stops making sense.

Windows laptop repair cost at a glance

Laptop repair pricing in Australia is quoted per job, and the range on any given repair is wide because the part, not the labour, drives the price. A budget HD screen and a 4K OLED gaming panel are the same two hours of work and a $400 difference in parts. These are the 2026 market ranges across Australian repairers:

The fees that aren't in the price list

Diagnostic fee: $50–$150. Most Australian shops charge it only if you decline the repair, and credit it against the job if you go ahead. Ask which applies before you hand the laptop over — on a $150 hinge adjustment, a non-credited $100 diagnostic changes the decision.

Express / same-day surcharge: $50–$100 in the major cities. On-site labour: $150–$205/hr for mobile technicians. Some shops waive call-out fees; most don't advertise them.

Screens: why the quotes differ so much

Screen replacement is the most-requested laptop repair in Australia and the one where quotes vary most. Four things move the number:

  • Panel spec. A 1366×768 TN panel is a $60 part. A 165Hz QHD gaming panel or a 4K OLED is $300–$500 wholesale before any labour.
  • Touch. On a touchscreen laptop, glass and LCD are usually bonded into one assembly — there's no cheap "just the glass" option, which is why touch models sit $100–$200 higher.
  • Bezel construction. Older laptops have a screwed bezel that pops off in five minutes. Modern thin-bezel ultrabooks are glued, adding an hour and the risk of cracking the housing.
  • Part availability. A three-year-old mainstream Dell or HP panel is in stock locally. A niche or discontinued model may be an import with a two-week wait and a premium.

The same pattern applies across brands: Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS and Acer parts all come from the same handful of panel makers, so brand matters less than spec. MacBooks are the real outlier — $450–$1,200 for a screen, because Apple's assemblies aren't available through general wholesale channels. We break that out separately in the MacBook repair cost guide.

Business laptops vs consumer laptops

A Dell Latitude, HP EliteBook or Lenovo ThinkPad is usually cheaper to repair than a same-priced consumer model, and that's not brand loyalty — it's design. Business lines use serviceable, widely-stocked parts: screwed-in batteries, modular keyboards, replaceable DC jacks, and service manuals the technician can actually follow. Consumer ultrabooks and gaming laptops increasingly glue batteries in place and integrate the keyboard into the top case, so a $60 keyboard becomes a $350 palmrest assembly. If repairability matters to you, that's the split to look at when buying — the same repair-first thinking that applies to phones.

When to stop repairing

The working rule across Australian repairers: if the repair exceeds about 60% of replacement cost and the laptop is over four years old, replace it. Applied to real numbers, that means:

SituationVerdict
$220 screen on a 2-year-old $1,400 ultrabookRepair — not close to the threshold
$180 battery on a 4-year-old $900 laptopRepair — batteries are wear items, the rest of the machine is fine
$250 SSD upgrade on a slow 5-year-old machineRepair — the single biggest speed gain per dollar
$600 motherboard on a 5-year-old $800 laptopReplace — well past the threshold, and other components are aging too
Liquid damage with a $400+ quote on an older machineUsually replace — corrosion faults recur months later

Two caveats. Screens, batteries and storage almost always justify repair regardless of age, because they're isolated wear items on an otherwise healthy machine. And if the data on the drive matters, pay for recovery before you decide anything — data recovery on a failed drive costs far more than pulling a working one out of a dead laptop.

Before you book a repair

  • Get the quote itemised in writing — parts and labour separately. A single number hides whether you're paying for a genuine panel or a generic one.
  • Ask what warranty covers the work. Three months is common; six is better.
  • Back up first. Any repair involving the board or storage carries data risk — the same rule we apply to phone repairs.
  • Know your rights. Australian Consumer Law guarantees apply to the repair itself, not just the original purchase — see our guide to repair warranties and consumer rights.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to replace a laptop screen in Australia?

$200–$450 for a standard HD or Full HD panel, and $380–$800 or more for gaming, 4K or OLED displays. Touchscreen models add $100–$200 because the glass and LCD are bonded into one assembly. MacBook screens are a separate tier at $450–$1,200.

Is it cheaper to repair a laptop or buy a new one?

The rule most Australian repairers use: if the quote exceeds about 60% of replacement cost and the laptop is more than four years old, replace it. Screens, batteries and SSD upgrades are the exceptions — they're isolated wear items and usually worth doing on any age of machine.

Will I be charged if I get a quote and don't proceed?

Often yes. Diagnostic fees run $50–$150 and are typically credited against the repair if you go ahead, but charged if you decline. Some walk-in shops quote free. Ask before you leave the laptop.

Why is a keyboard replacement sometimes $150 and sometimes $450?

It depends on whether the keyboard is a separate part. On most business laptops it unclips as a module. On thin ultrabooks and many consumer models it's riveted into the top case, so the repair means replacing the whole palmrest assembly — parts and labour both jump.
