How Long Does Phone Repair Take?

Published 22 June 2026 · Written by RepairRange Editorial · 5 min read

You need your phone fixed, but you also need your phone. How long are you actually going to be without it? Here are realistic timeframes for every common repair — at a professional shop and as a DIY repair.

Screen Replacement: 30–60 Minutes

This is the most common phone repair. A skilled technician replaces an iPhone screen in about 30 minutes and a Samsung Galaxy screen in 45–60 minutes. The difference is adhesive — iPhones use pentalobe screws and clips that are relatively quick to undo, while Samsung screens are glued to the frame and require heat separation.

Most independent shops carry common screens in stock, so there is no waiting for parts. Walk in, leave the phone, go grab a coffee, and it is done when you get back.

DIY screen replacement: 1.5–3 hours your first time

The extra time is all preparation and caution — watching the video guide, carefully heating adhesive, managing tiny screws, and testing before reassembly. After your first repair, subsequent screen swaps take under an hour.

Battery Replacement: 20–45 Minutes

Battery replacement is one of the fastest professional repairs. iPhone batteries take 20–30 minutes — remove the screen, disconnect the old battery, apply the new one, reconnect, and test. Samsung batteries take 30–45 minutes because the back glass needs to be removed first (heated and pried off), which adds time.

For DIY, budget 1–2 hours your first time. The process is straightforward but requires patience with adhesive removal. SelfRepairKit's battery replacement guide walks you through the full process.

Charging Port: 30–60 Minutes

Charging port replacement involves opening the phone, disconnecting the old port flex cable assembly, and installing a new one. The process is similar in complexity to a battery replacement. Samsung Galaxy ports are considered a straightforward repair — DIY kits are available for $79. iPhone ports are more complex and best left to professionals.

Water Damage: 2–4 Hours (Sometimes Overnight)

Water damage treatment is the most time-intensive common repair. The technician needs to fully disassemble the phone, clean every component in an ultrasonic bath, dry and inspect under magnification, and reassemble. This process takes 2–4 hours minimum.

If the phone needs to sit in the ultrasonic cleaner for an extended cycle, or if components need to be tested individually, the shop may ask you to leave the phone overnight. Read our full water damage emergency guide for what to do immediately after your phone gets wet.

What Slows Down Repairs?

Parts availability

Common models (iPhone 12–16, Galaxy S22–S25) are almost always in stock at busy shops. Older models, OPPO, Google Pixel, or less common variants may need to be ordered — adding 1–3 business days. Call ahead and ask if they have the part before visiting.

Queue length

A busy Saturday at a shopping centre repair kiosk means your 30-minute repair might not start for 2 hours. Street-front shops and appointment-based services typically have shorter wait times. Ask for an estimated start time when you drop off.

Adhesive curing

After reassembly, waterproof adhesive needs time to cure for full seal strength. Some shops hand the phone back immediately and advise you not to submerge it for 24 hours. Others hold the phone for an extra 30 minutes under pressure clamps for a stronger seal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does phone screen replacement take?

Professional screen replacement takes 30–60 minutes. iPhones typically 30–45 minutes, Samsung 45–60 minutes. Most shops offer walk-in, wait-while-you-wait service.

Can I get my phone repaired same day?

Yes. Most independent repair shops offer same-day service for screens, batteries, and charging ports. Walk-in service is standard — no appointment needed at most shops.

How long does battery replacement take?

Professional battery replacement takes 20–45 minutes. iPhones 20–30 minutes, Samsung 30–45 minutes due to back glass removal.

How long does DIY phone repair take?

First-time DIY screen replacement takes 1.5–3 hours, battery replacement 1–2 hours. Experienced DIYers halve these times. The extra time is spent on the video guide and careful adhesive work.
