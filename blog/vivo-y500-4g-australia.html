Introducing the vivo Y500 4G: A New Contender in the Australian Smartphone Market

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 07, 2026

Overview of the vivo Y500 4G

Hot on the heels of its recent teaser, vivo has officially unveiled the Y500 4G for the Australian market. With an appealing set of specifications, this device is set to make waves among tech enthusiasts and casual users alike.

Display & Design

The vivo Y500 boasts a stunning 6.83-inch AMOLED touchscreen, delivering a resolution of 1260x2800 pixels. One of its standout features is the impressive 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth scrolling and enhanced gaming experiences. Additionally, with a peak brightness of 5,000 nits, users can enjoy vibrant colours even under direct sunlight.

Power-Packed Performance

Under the hood, the Y500 is powered by the Unisoc T7300 SoC. Coupled with 8GB of RAM, this smartphone provides robust multitasking capabilities, perfect for those who demand efficiency in their daily tasks. Users can choose between 128GB and 256GB of internal storage, accommodating plenty of apps, photos, and videos.

Long-lasting Battery Life

One of the most notable features of the vivo Y500 is its mammoth 8,100 mAh battery. Designed to keep up with the busy lifestyles of Australians, this battery capacity means you won’t be searching for a charger by midday. The Y500 promises extended usage, perfect for streaming, gaming, and social media browsing without the constant worry of running out of power.

Why Choose the vivo Y500?

The combination of a high-refresh-rate display, powerful processor, and an enormous battery makes the vivo Y500 a formidable player in the smartphone market. Whether you’re capturing moments with its camera or enjoying your favourite content on its vibrant screen, the Y500 is tailored for performance.
