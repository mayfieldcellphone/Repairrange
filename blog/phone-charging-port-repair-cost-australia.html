A phone charging port repair in Australia costs between $72 and $189 at an independent repairer depending on your model — with authorised repairs running $135–$269. But before you book, there are two free fixes that solve a surprisingly large share of "won't charge" problems.

Try these free fixes first

A large share of "won't charge" cases are never actually a dead port. Work through our charging port troubleshooting guide, but the short version is:

  • Clean the port. Lint and pocket fluff is the number one cause. Use a soft plastic or wooden pick (never metal), or a dry toothbrush, with the phone off.
  • Swap the cable and brick. Cables fail far more often than ports. Try a different known-good cable, and try slow vs fast chargers.
  • Restart, then test wireless charging. If wireless charging works, the battery and charging circuitry are fine — it points to the port itself.
  • If you see a moisture warning, wait. A "moisture detected" alert means the port thinks it's wet — give it 24 hours in a dry spot before doing anything else.

Signs it's actually the port (not the cable)

  • Phone only charges when the cable is held at a certain angle
  • Charging works with some cables but not others, and none of the cables are damaged
  • Charging is slow or keeps connecting and disconnecting
  • No wired charging at all, but wireless charging works fine

If you're hitting two or more of these, a port replacement is likely the fix — and at $72–$189 it's usually cheaper than you expect.

Is it worth repairing or replacing?

Port repairs are among the cheapest fixes on the board, so on any phone under about four years old it's almost always worth repairing rather than replacing. FAQ

Common questions

Is a charging port repair covered by warranty?

Usually no if the port failed from wear, debris or physical damage. But if the port stops working on its own early in the phone's life with no impact or water exposure, it can be a manufacturing defect claimable under Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees — worth raising with the seller or manufacturer first before paying for a repair.

Can I charge wirelessly instead of fixing the port?

Yes — every iPhone from the 8 onwards and most modern Androids support wireless charging, so it's a legitimate workaround while you decide. It isn't a fix for the underlying port issue, and it's slower than a cable, but it buys you time without spending money.

How long does a charging port repair take?

Most independent repairers complete a charging port replacement same-day, typically in 30–60 minutes once they have the part in stock. It's one of the faster phone repairs, which is another reason it's usually worth doing rather than replacing the phone.

Why does my phone only charge at a certain angle?

That's the classic symptom of a worn or lint-filled port, or a bent cable connector. If holding the cable at an angle makes charging work, the port is usually the problem — try a careful clean first, then a port replacement if that doesn't help.
