The Magic V6 is available in a single configuration of 16GB RAM and 512GB storage, ensuring you have more than enough power and space for your digital life. This premium smartphone is priced at AUD 1,700, making it a competitive choice against other flagship devices on the market.

What the V6's arrival means for foldable repair pricing in Australia

Honor's move into the Australian market puts another foldable option in local stores — and foldables carry the most expensive repair bills in the industry, with inner screens commonly quoted at $599–$899. The Magic V6's arrival matters for repair shopping for two reasons: more devices in circulation means more parts in the supply chain, which slowly normalises pricing, and it gives Australian buyers a genuine third option beyond Samsung and Google in the foldable space. If you're considering one, factor the repair cost into the purchase — a foldable's screen is a wear item, not a one-off accident risk.
