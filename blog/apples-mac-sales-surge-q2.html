Apple's Mac Sales Surge While Competition Declines in Q2

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 10, 2026

Apple Leads the Pack in Q2 Mac Sales

The second quarter of this year has showcased a dramatic shift in the computer market, with Apple emerging as the undeniable victor. According to a recent report from IDC, Apple's Mac shipments have surged by 10.1% compared to the same period last year, making it the only brand to gain market share amidst a sector-wide decline.

Impressive Shipment Figures

In total, Apple shipped approximately 6.7 million Macs in Q2, cementing its position at the forefront of consumer preferences. This performance not only highlights Apple's robust product appeal but also reflects a growing trend among users favouring high-quality, reliable laptops.

What This Means for Australian Consumers

For Australians considering an upgrade, this growth is promising news. As competition among PC makers continues to stagnate or decline, those seeking dependable, innovative technology may find that Apple's offerings stand out even more. With options ranging from MacBook Airs to MacBook Pros, there’s a platform for every need, from casual browsing to professional-grade work.
