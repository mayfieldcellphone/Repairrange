All You Need to Know About the New Nothing Ear (3a) in Australia

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 08, 2026

Introduction

In an exciting announcement that has tech enthusiasts buzzing across Australia, UK-based Nothing has unveiled its latest pair of true wireless earbuds, the Nothing Ear (3a). Released alongside their new flagship smartphone, the Nothing Phone (4b), these earbuds promise a range of features that cater to the everyday audio needs of Australians.

Design that Fits Everyone

The Nothing Ear (3a) sports a sleek in-ear design, complete with silicone ear tips that are now available in an extra-small (XS) size. This addition ensures that users of all ear shapes and sizes can enjoy a snug and comfortable fit, enhancing both sound quality and user experience.

Stellar Sound Quality

Equipped with a robust 12mm dynamic driver, the Ear (3a) is set to deliver impressive audio performance. Whether you’re pumping out your favourite Aussie rock anthems or diving into the latest podcasts, you’ll appreciate the clarity and depth these earbuds bring to the table.

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) for Superior Listening

One of the standout features of the Nothing Ear (3a) is its Active Noise Cancellation capability. Whether you're commuting in the city, working in a bustling café, or simply enjoying your home sanctuary, ANC technology effectively minimises background noise, allowing you to immerse yourself fully in your audio experience.

Unmatched Battery Life

With up to 42 hours of total battery life, the Nothing Ear (3a) is perfect for those long days when you're constantly on the go. With the charging case in tow, you can enjoy uninterrupted listening for days at a time without worrying about your earbuds running out of juice.

Conclusion

The Nothing Ear (3a) earbuds promise a blend of style, performance, and longevity. Whether you’re a tech connoisseur or someone who simply enjoys quality audio, these earbuds set a new standard in the true wireless market. Keep an eye out for them!
