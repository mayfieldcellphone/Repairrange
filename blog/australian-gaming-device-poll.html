What's Your Go-To Gaming Device?

The gaming scene in Australia has seen some ups and downs in recent years. From the impact of COVID-19 on hardware availability to the booming crypto market snatching up GPUs, gamers have faced significant challenges. However, despite these hurdles, the passion for gaming remains strong down under.

Current Trends in Gaming Devices

As we look around, what devices are Australians gravitating towards for their gaming escapades? Smartphones, tablets, PCs, and consoles are all competing for attention. With prices on the rise due to higher production costs and demand, especially with smartphone manufacturers bumping up their prices, it's worthwhile to explore what’s popular.

Smartphones: Gaming on the Go

Smartphones have surged in popularity as portable gaming devices. Titles like Call of Duty Mobile and Genshin Impact have transformed everyday phones into powerful gaming platforms. And let’s be honest, with the convenience of gaming in your pocket, it's no wonder many Aussies prefer this option.

PCs vs. Consoles: The Ongoing Debate

For serious gamers, the debate between PCs and consoles remains fierce. PCs offer customizable experiences and superior graphics, while consoles are all about ease of use. With the recent launch of the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X, many players are excited to immerse themselves in cutting-edge gaming. Which camp do you fall into?
