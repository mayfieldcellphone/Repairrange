RR

What We Know About the New iPhone 18 Pro Max

With the anticipation building for Apple's latest flagship, the iPhone 18 Pro Max, a recent leak has revealed that this model might be packing on the grams—and the millimetres. Prolific tech leaker Ice Universe has indicated that the new device will be noticeably thicker than its predecessor, by about 2mm. For anyone in Australia contemplating an upgrade, here’s what you need to know.

Chunkier Than Before

As smartphone designs evolve, there’s often a push for sleeker profiles, yet it seems the iPhone 18 Pro Max is taking a different approach. While the specifics around its thickness are still to be confirmed, the increase does suggest Apple is prioritising enhanced battery life and possibly a more robust camera system. How will this extra beef feel in hand? If you’ve come to rely on slim, lightweight designs, you might find this shift a bit chunky.

Weight Matters

Together with the increase in thickness, details around the weight of the device have also surfaced. While exact numbers are yet to be verified, reports indicate that the heft of the iPhone 18 Pro Max will feel notably different when compared to its lighter predecessors. This could raise questions about usability, particularly for those who enjoy using their phones one-handed.

Implications for Australian Consumers

For those of us in Oz, consider how this potential weight and thickness change could influence everyday usage. If you’re often on the go or value portability, this new chunky design could be a considerable factor. If you’re excited about the upgraded specifications yet concerned about the feel in your hand, it might be worth testing it out in-store once it hits the shelves.
