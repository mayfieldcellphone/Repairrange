The Rise of Smartwatch Shipments in Australia: Q1 2026 Analysis

By RepairRange · Independently researched pricing · Updated 15 August 2026

Published on June 19, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

Understanding the Growth in Smartwatch Shipments

According to the recent report from Counterpoint, the smartwatch market has shown a commendable 4% growth in Q1 2026 compared to the same quarter last year. This increase comes on the back of a sustained recovery observed throughout 2025, after a tumultuous 2024. Apple's innovative approach to wearables has significantly contributed to this growth.

Apple: The Dominant Force in the Market

Apple continues to be the leader in the smartwatch arena, leveraging a combination of cutting-edge technology and consumer demand. The introduction of new features and improved integration with the iOS ecosystem has attracted more users, particularly in Australia. The Apple Watch Series continues to be a favorite among Aussie consumers, due to its functionality, design, and brand reputation.

✅ Good to know: The seamless integration of Apple wearables with other devices enhances user experience, making them a popular choice for Australians looking for convenience in their tech.

Competitive Landscape: Who's Catching Up?

While Apple leads the pack, other brands are also experiencing growth in their smartwatch segments. Companies like Samsung and Garmin are making strides, offering competitive features and pricing to entice consumers. The advancements in fitness tracking, health monitoring, and battery life have significantly increased consumer interest in these products. As Australian consumers become more health-conscious, the demand for smartwatches is expected to grow.

Market Insights and Consumer Preferences

The trend in Australia reflects a broader global movement towards smart wearable technology. Analysts indicate that consumers are increasingly prioritising health functionalities, such as heart rate monitoring and exercise tracking. As these features become standard, brands that innovate and provide superior user experiences are expected to thrive.
