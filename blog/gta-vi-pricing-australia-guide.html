GTA VI Pricing Revealed: What Aussies Need to Know

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 25, 2026

GTA VI Pre-Orders Start Soon

Get ready, Aussie gamers! The buzz is all around as Rockstar Games has confirmed that pre-orders for the much-anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI) will kick off on June 25. For many, this is the moment they’ve been waiting for, especially considering the iconic status of the series.

What’s the Price Tag?

The main question on everyone's mind has finally been answered: how much will GTA VI set you back? Rockstar has assured fans that the pricing will be reasonable—well, here’s what they mean by that.

Are you ready? The Standard Edition is going to be priced at $80 AUD, which puts it in a respectable range for a title of this calibre. If you’re tempted to splurge a little more, the Deluxe Edition will come in at $100 AUD. Both editions promise to offer plenty of value, which should appease even the most discerning gamers.

What Can We Expect?

GTA VI is expected to deliver immersive gameplay, stunning graphics, and an expansive open-world experience that the franchise is known for. With a rich storyline and dynamic characters, it looks poised to be a game-changer in the series.
