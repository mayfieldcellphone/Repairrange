Amazfit Unveils Beta Feature for iOS Notification Forwarding: What Aussies Need to Know

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 30, 2026

Introducing Amazfit's Notification Forwarding Feature

For Aussies keen on maximising their smart wearable experience, Amazfit has something exciting in store. The tech company has officially launched a beta program focusing on iOS Notification Forwarding. This innovative feature aims to streamline notifications from your iPhone directly to your Amazfit device.

What is Notification Forwarding?

With this new feature, Amazfit users can expect real-time notifications from their iPhones delivered right to their smartwatches. Whether it's calls, messages, or app alerts, the integration makes it easier to stay connected without having to constantly reach for your phone. Note, however, that participation in this beta is exclusive to users in certain regions.

How to Participate in the Beta Test

If you're an Amazfit user located in an eligible area, you can sign up for beta access through the Amazfit app. Keep an eye on your device’s settings to enable this feature once you're enrolled. It’s a straightforward process, but you'll want to make sure your watch is up to date with the latest software to enjoy the full benefits.

Why This Matters for Aussie Users

This addition is set to enhance how you interact with notifications while maintaining your active lifestyle. Instead of fishing your phone out of your pocket or bag during a walk or workout, you can quickly glance at your wrist. It’s all about convenience, especially for those busy Aussies juggling work and play.
