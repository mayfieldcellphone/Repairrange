Mark your calendars, tech enthusiasts! Samsung is gearing up for its next Unpacked event slated for July 22, 2023, in London. This much-anticipated showcase will reveal the latest in their flagship smartphone line-up: the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, and the Galaxy Z Flip8.
