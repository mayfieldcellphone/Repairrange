Say Goodbye to Physical Discs: Sony's Shift to Digital Only by 2028

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 02, 2026

The End of an Era: Sony's Bold Move Towards Digital Gaming

In a move that has sent shockwaves through the gaming community, Sony has declared that it will cease production of physical disc games for the PlayStation by January 2028. This announcement follows Rockstar's recent news about the upcoming Grand Theft Auto VI being a digital-only title, signalling a broader trend towards digital media in the gaming world.

The Future is Digital

As more players favour the convenience of downloadable games over traditional physical copies, Sony's decision reflects a significant pivot in the gaming landscape. With internet speeds improving across Australia and digital game libraries expanding exponentially, it’s clear that many gamers prefer the convenience of having their entire library available at the click of a button.

What This Means for Aussie Gamers

Australian gamers may need to start preparing for this inevitable transition. While digital platforms provide instant access to games, it's crucial to consider various factors such as data limits and the reliability of internet connections, especially in more remote areas.

While the end of physical discs might feel like a loss, embracing this digital shift can also open up new possibilities for gamers in Australia. The benefits of faster downloads, easier updates, and space-saving options are just some of the advantages that come with going digital. As we move towards 2028, the focus on digital gaming will likely increase, making it essential for gamers to adapt to this change.
