Exciting News for Aussie Apple Fans: Samsung Begins Production of OLED Panels for the Foldable iPhone

By RepairRange Editorial Team • June 23, 2026

The Foldable iPhone: A Game Changer on the Horizon

Apple's long-rumoured foldable iPhone is finally taking shape, with the latest reports suggesting that it could be officially unveiled to the world in September. This innovative device, likely to be branded as the iPhone Ultra, is generating a lot of buzz in the Australian tech community.

Samsung Display: The Chosen Supplier

In a significant move that underscores the collaboration between two tech giants, Samsung Display has reportedly kicked off production of OLED panels specifically designed for the foldable iPhone. For Aussies excited about the technological advancements of this upcoming device, this partnership means high-quality displays that promise vibrant colours and enhanced durability.

While details are still under wraps, we anticipate that the iPhone Ultra will feature cutting-edge specifications and a versatile design that takes full advantage of its foldable capabilities. This could mark a pivotal moment not just for Apple, but for the entire smartphone market in Australia.
