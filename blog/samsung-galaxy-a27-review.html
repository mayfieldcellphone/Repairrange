Samsung Galaxy A27 Review: What’s New Down Under?

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 02, 2026

Unveiling the Samsung Galaxy A27

Samsung has officially launched its latest budget-friendly offering, the Galaxy A27, which is now available to Australian consumers. This new addition to the Galaxy lineup promises to deliver impressive features without breaking the bank.

A Fresh Design: The Punch Hole Display

One of the most noticeable changes in the Galaxy A27 is its display. Gone is the teardrop notch from the previous model; instead, Samsung has opted for a modern punch hole notch. This design not only enhances the aesthetic appeal but also provides a larger screen-to-body ratio, making it ideal for media consumption and gaming.

Performance Upgrade with Snapdragon 6 Gen 3

Under the hood, the Galaxy A27 shifts gears with the introduction of the 4nm Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset, which replaces the older 5nm Exynos 1380 found in its predecessor. This new processor promises improved performance and efficiency, ensuring that users can multitask seamlessly and enjoy a smoother experience overall.

The Essential Upgrade for Aussie Users

With the Galaxy A27 now available in Australia, it’s about time we consider how this device enhances everyday life. Whether you're into photography, social media, or gaming, the upgrades in both display and performance suggest that the Galaxy A27 is set to impress.
