Steam Deck & ROG Ally Repair Cost in Australia (2026)

By RepairRange · how we price · Updated 13 September 2026

Handheld repair is the one category where almost nobody publishes prices. Here's every Australian figure we could verify, which faults are quote-only, and how to make quotes comparable.

The honest answer: almost nobody in Australia publishes handheld prices

We build this site on published price data, and gaming handhelds are the one category where that data mostly doesn't exist. Search for Steam Deck or ROG Ally repair pricing in Australia and you'll find a lot of "request a free quote" buttons and very few numbers. That's not evasion on the shops' part — the volume is low, the parts supply is inconsistent, and two Steam Decks with the same symptom can need very different work. But it does mean you walk in without a reference point, which is the worst position to negotiate from.

So this page does something different from our other cost guides: it lists every Australian handheld repair price we could actually verify as published, says plainly which faults are quote-only, and gives you the questions that turn a vague quote into a comparable one.

Stick drift: the fault worth spending extra on

Drift is to handhelds what it is to the Nintendo Switch — the single most common complaint, and the one with two very different fixes. A like-for-like replacement puts back the same wearing potentiometer module that drifted in the first place. A Hall-effect stick uses magnets instead of physical contacts and simply doesn't wear the same way.

At around $89 to install customer-supplied Hall-effect sticks, the upgrade path costs little more than a standard swap and ends the problem permanently. Two caveats: you buy the sticks yourself (they're widely available online), and the common aftermarket kits are not compatible with the OLED Steam Deck — check your model before ordering.

ROG Ally and the other Windows handhelds

ASUS ROG Ally, Lenovo Legion Go and the rest sit in an awkward spot: they're Windows PCs in a handheld shell, so the failure modes look like laptop faults — screens, batteries, charging ports, thermal problems — but almost no independent shop stocks the parts. In practice that leaves the manufacturer's Australian service channel as the first stop while the device is in warranty, and a board-level specialist after that. Ally owners in particular should note that the widely-reported microSD reader failures are a thermal design issue rather than accidental damage, which is a warranty conversation, not a paid repair.

How to get a quote you can actually compare

Since you're dealing with quotes rather than price lists, make them comparable. Ask every shop the same five things:

  • Is the diagnostic free, and is it credited if I proceed? Free diagnosis is normal in this category — don't pay for one without checking.
  • Is that price parts-inclusive? Some handheld quotes are labour-only with the part billed on arrival.
  • New part or donor board? Both are legitimate; the price should differ.
  • What's the warranty on the work? Three months is common, six is better.
  • Have you done this model before? Fair question on hardware this niche, and the answer tells you a lot.

Repair or replace?

Handhelds hold value well and cost a lot to replace, so the threshold sits high — most single-fault repairs on this page are worth doing. The line to watch is the motherboard: once a quote passes roughly $250 on a device that's several years old, you're at the point where a used replacement unit competes, especially given how much cheaper the previous generation gets once a new model lands. Our repair-or-replace framework applies unchanged — 60% of replacement cost is the number.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does Steam Deck repair cost in Australia?

It depends heavily on the fault, and most shops quote rather than publish. Verified published prices include roughly $175 for a cleaning and overheating service, $185 for an HDMI port, $205 for storage repair and $220 for motherboard work. Screens, batteries and charging ports are quote-only — budget $180–$350.

What actually fixes Steam Deck stick drift?

A standard replacement uses the same potentiometer module that wore out, so drift tends to return. Hall-effect sticks use magnets with no physical contact and don't wear the same way — around $89 to have customer-supplied sticks fitted. Check compatibility first: common kits don't fit the OLED model.

Can a normal phone repair shop fix my Steam Deck or ROG Ally?

Usually not. Handhelds are a separate specialisation with their own parts supply, and several Australian specialists are mail-in only or have paused bookings. Always confirm a shop takes your specific model before sending it.

Is a free diagnostic really free?

In this category it generally is — several Australian handheld repairers advertise no-cost assessment with no obligation if you decline the work. That's different from phone repair, where a declined repair often attracts a fee. Confirm it before handing the device over.
