Oppo's Widescreen Foldable: New Leaks Hint at an Aussie Game-Changer (and Apple Challenger!)

Published on June 11, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

The Aussie Tech Horizon: Oppo's Bold Play for the Foldable Crown

G'day, tech enthusiasts! Remember those whispers earlier this year about Oppo secretly toiling away on a wide-format foldable phone, aiming squarely at Apple's rumoured, über-premium 'iPhone Ultra'? Well, those whispers are now turning into a full-blown roar, with a recent deluge of leaked information giving us a much clearer picture of what this device might entail.

It seems Oppo isn't just looking to dip its toes into the foldable market; they're ready to make a massive splash, potentially redefining what a premium foldable experience can be for us Aussies. For an industry often dominated by a couple of major players, a strong, innovative contender like this from Oppo could be exactly what the doctor ordered, bringing more choice and pushing boundaries in the always-evolving mobile phone landscape down under.

Diving Deep Down Under: What the Latest Leaks Reveal

The latest intel, courtesy of the ever-reliable Digital Chat Station (DCS) on Weibo, paints a compelling picture of Oppo's forthcoming foldable. And let's be honest, it sounds like a absolute beast! Here’s a quick rundown of the juicy specs making waves:

  • A Grand Display: Prepare for a serious visual treat. The internal folding screen is rumoured to be a whopping 8.01-inch panel – that's some serious digital real estate! It's expected to be a high-resolution Samsung E6 display, promising vibrant colours and deep contrasts. The external screen is no slouch either, coming in at a respectable 6.5 inches. Imagine trying to protect that much glass!
  • Under the Bonnet Power: Powering this marvel is expected to be Qualcomm's cutting-edge Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor. This means top-tier performance, whether you're gaming, multitasking, or just zipping through your day-to-day apps.
  • Enduring Energy: A large, high-res screen needs a robust power source, and Oppo looks set to deliver with a battery capacity estimated to be around 5000mAh. That should get you through even the busiest of Aussie days.
  • Blazing Fast Charging: Low on juice? Not for long! The device is tipped to feature an incredible 100W wired charging capability, along with a speedy 50W wireless charging option. You'll be back in action quicker than you can say 'barbie'.
  • Click-Worthy Cameras: Photography buffs will be pleased to hear about a potential 50MP main camera, complemented by a capable 3x telephoto lens. Expect sharp, detailed shots, even if you're capturing our stunning Aussie landscapes.
  • Sleek Design Philosophy: Despite its formidable specs and folding nature, the device is reportedly designed to be both thinner and lighter than previous foldables, which is fantastic news for portability and ergonomics.

When Can We Expect This Aussie Powerhouse?

So, when might we get our hands on this potential game-changer? The leaks suggest a launch window somewhere in the second quarter of 2024 – so, between April and June next year. This timing is particularly interesting as it could coincide with or precede the rumoured launch of Apple's own 'Ultra' device, setting the stage for a fascinating head-to-head battle for premium smartphone supremacy.

For us in Australia, it means more exciting options hitting the market, pushing innovation and giving consumers greater choice when investing in a high-end mobile device. Get ready for some serious competition in the flagship phone arena!
