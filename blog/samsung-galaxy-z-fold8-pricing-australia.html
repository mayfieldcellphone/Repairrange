Anticipated Pricing for Samsung's Latest Devices in Australia: Galaxy Z Fold8, Flip8, and More

RR

Upcoming Samsung Devices: A Quick Overview

Excitement is building in Australia as Samsung prepares to unveil its latest innovations, including the Galaxy Z Fold8, Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Galaxy Z Flip8, and the new Galaxy Watch9 and Watch Ultra 2. This much-anticipated Unpacked event is set for July 22 in London, and leaks have already begun to shed light on the expected pricing.

Expected Pricing for Samsung’s New Lineup

While precise Australian pricing is yet to be confirmed, European leaks suggest that these devices may come with a higher price tag than previous models. Here’s a summary of the leaked European prices, which can give us a rough idea of what to expect down under:

  • Galaxy Z Fold8: Approx. €1,900
  • Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra: Approx. €2,200
  • Galaxy Z Flip8: Approx. €1,100
  • Galaxy Watch9: Approx. €500
  • Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: Approx. €700

With currency exchange rates and local taxes, we can reasonably anticipate these figures to translate into slightly higher values in Australia.

What These Devices Bring to the Table

Samsungs’ Galaxy Z series has been a game changer in the smartphone market, promoting a foldable design that enhances user experience. Fans can expect refined features in the Fold8 series, including improved multitasking capabilities and display quality. The Flip8 is likely to maintain its status as a popular choice for users wanting a compact device with flair.

Similarly, the new Galaxy Watch9 series promises to deliver advanced health monitoring features and stylish designs to keep up with the latest trends in wearable technology.
