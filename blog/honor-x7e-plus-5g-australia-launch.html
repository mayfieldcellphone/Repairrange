Get Ready for the Honor X7e Plus 5G in Australia!

Published on June 13, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

Introducing the Honor X7e Plus 5G: A New Contender

The tech landscape in Australia is buzzing with anticipation as Honor gears up to expand its smartphone lineup. Following the recent launch of the Honor X7e 4G, enthusiasts have been eager to know if a 5G variant is on the horizon. The answer is a resounding yes! The newly certified Honor X7e Plus 5G has received the green light for sale in the UAE, hinting that an Australian release could be just around the corner.

What We Know So Far

The Honor X7e Plus 5G has been spotted in various databases, indicating that it’s making significant strides in the certification process. Although detailed specifications are still under wraps, here's what we can speculate:

  • 5G Connectivity: As its name suggests, this model will support 5G networks, promising ultra-fast browsing and streaming experiences.
  • Design and Build: Expect a sleek design that aligns with Honor's aesthetic, possibly featuring vibrant colour options and a modern display.
  • Camera Capabilities: Honor is known for its impressive camera technology, and the X7e Plus 5G is likely to deliver enhanced photography experiences.

Potential Market Impact in Australia

The introduction of the Honor X7e Plus 5G in the Australian market could shake things up. As consumers increasingly seek devices that offer both performance and affordability, this smartphone could cater to a growing demand for budget-friendly 5G options.

With the cost of living constantly rising, many Aussies may prefer to invest in devices that don’t break the bank while providing top-tier specifications.
