AirPods Repair Cost in Australia (2026): Battery, Single Buds & Cases

By RepairRange · how we price · Updated 13 September 2026

Lost one bud? You don't need a new set. What every AirPods replacement and battery service costs in Australia — and the AppleCare+ clause that cuts it to $45.

AirPods aren't repaired — they're replaced, one bud at a time

This is the thing that catches people out. There is no "AirPods repair" in the sense that a phone gets a new screen: the buds are sealed units, glued and ultrasonically welded, with no serviceable parts inside. What Apple and its Authorised Service Providers actually sell is replacement — a new left bud, a new right bud, or a new charging case, priced individually. That's better news than it sounds, because it means losing one AirPod doesn't mean buying a new set.

Each piece is sold separately. Left bud, right bud and case are three independent purchases. A replacement bud pairs with your existing case and the other bud — you don't need matching serials.

Two more line items worth knowing: replacement ear tips are $10 each or $15 a pair, and a standard assessment is $59 — credited toward the repair if you proceed, charged if you walk away. Warranty and AppleCare+ assessments are free.

The AppleCare+ number that changes everything

If you have AppleCare+, a damaged bud is replaced for $45 instead of $125–$149, and a battery that has dropped below 80% capacity is serviced free. That's the clause most people forget they're entitled to.

Lost is not damaged. AppleCare+ covers accidental damage, not loss. A bud that fell down a drain is a full-price replacement ($125–$149) whether or not you're covered. Only a bud you still physically hold qualifies for the $45 rate.

Battery service: worth it or not?

At $95 per bud, replacing both batteries costs $190 — and that's where the maths gets uncomfortable, because standard AirPods sell new for not much more than that. The decision usually goes:

  • One bud with a dead battery: $95 is clearly worth it. You keep the case and the good bud.
  • Both buds dying on standard AirPods: $190 against a new pair at roughly $200–$300. Replace, unless the model is recent.
  • Both buds dying on AirPods Pro: $190 against a new Pro set well north of $350. Service them.
  • AirPods Max at $169: almost always worth it — the headphones cost several times that, and the rest of the hardware outlasts the cell comfortably.

Batteries in earbuds are consumables, exactly like phone batteries: two to three years of daily charge cycles is a normal service life, not a defect.

Why third-party AirPods repair barely exists in Australia

Walk into an independent repair shop with a cracked iPhone screen and you'll get a quote in thirty seconds. Walk in with AirPods and most will send you to Apple. The reason is construction, not reluctance: the shells are sealed with adhesive and ultrasonic welds designed never to reopen, so there's no non-destructive way in and no supply of genuine internal parts. Some shops will attempt a battery swap on older models, but the case usually shows the damage afterwards and the water resistance is gone for good. For AirPods specifically, the authorised channel is the practical one — the opposite of our usual advice on Apple vs third-party repair.

Before you pay for anything

  • Clean them first. A bud that's gone quiet is more often blocked with earwax than broken. The mesh responds to a dry soft brush — no liquid, no compressed air.
  • Reset the pair. Hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber, then re-pair. This fixes a surprising share of "one bud won't connect" faults for free.
  • Check the case, not the buds. If both buds die fast, the case battery is often the real culprit — it's the part doing the most charge cycles.
  • Check coverage before you book. Your serial tells you whether warranty or AppleCare+ applies, and it turns a $149 quote into a $45 one.
  • Water damage: no AirPods model is rated for submersion. Dry it fully before testing and don't charge a wet case — the same rule as a water-damaged phone.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I buy just one AirPod to replace a lost one?

Yes. Left bud, right bud and charging case are sold separately — $125 for standard AirPods, $149 for AirPods Pro. The replacement pairs with your existing case and other bud; serials do not need to match.

How much is an AirPods battery replacement in Australia?

$95 per earbud at an Apple Authorised Service Provider, or $169 for AirPods Max. With AppleCare+, battery service is free once capacity drops below 80%.

Will a phone repair shop fix my AirPods?

Most won't, and that's a construction problem rather than an unwillingness. The shells are glued and ultrasonically welded shut with no genuine internal parts available, so opening them is destructive and voids any water resistance. AirPods are one of the few devices where the authorised channel is the practical option.

Is it cheaper to replace both batteries or buy new AirPods?

For standard AirPods, $190 for two battery services sits close to a new pair, so replacing usually wins unless the set is recent. For AirPods Pro and AirPods Max the service is clearly cheaper than new hardware.
