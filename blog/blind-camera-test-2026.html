Blind Camera Test of the Best Flagships in 2026

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 05, 2026

Introducing the Ultimate Smartphone Camera Showdown

In the ever-evolving world of mobile technology, the competition to create the best camera-equipped flagship is fierce. This year, we’ve gathered three of the most talked-about devices of 2026: the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra, Google Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max. 

The How and Why of Our Blind Test

To provide an unbiased comparison, we partnered with our mates from Versus for a thorough blind video test. With Will from our video crew and Olive from Versus behind the lens, we aimed to strip away brand bias and let the quality of each camera speak for itself. The results might just surprise you!

The Contenders

  • Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra: Known for its advanced zoom capabilities and vibrant colour accuracy.
  • Google Pixel 10 Pro XL: Lauded for its exceptional low-light performance and AI enhancements.
  • Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max: Renowned for its true-to-life colour reproduction and consistent performance.

What We Tested

We subjected these flagships to a series of scenarios that showcased their strengths—from daylight landscapes to dimly lit indoor shots. Each device recorded video clips that were later reviewed anonymously, ensuring a level playing field for evaluation.

The Results: Which Device Reigned Supreme?

While we won’t spoil the surprise, the blind test revealed some unexpected favourites among our testers. Each camera brought something unique to the table, making it crucial for potential buyers to consider what features are most important to them.
