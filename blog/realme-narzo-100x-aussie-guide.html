Your Guide to the Upcoming Realme Narzo 100x: Specs, Price, and Launch Info

RR
By RepairRange Editorial Team • July 10, 2026

Excitement Builds for the Realme Narzo 100x

Realme is set to introduce a new contender in the smartphone market with the Narzo 100x, slated for launch on July 15. For tech enthusiasts and budget-conscious buyers alike, this device may just be the next big thing.

Standout Features of the Narzo 100x

The Narzo 100x is drawing attention not only for its sleek design but particularly for its impressive 8,000 mAh battery. Realme is touting this battery as the "segment's biggest", promising an extended usage time ideal for both everyday tasks and gaming.

Price Range and Value for Money

If you're curious about how much the Narzo 100x will set you back, it is expected to fall within a price range of AUD 300 to AUD 400. For this price point, the combination of a robust battery and decent specifications make it a compelling choice for those keeping an eye on their budget.

What to Expect on Launch Day

As we approach the launch date, consumers can prepare for potential offers and promotions that Realme typically extends during new releases. Additionally, keep an eye out for reviews focusing on the Narzo 100x's performance, especially regarding the user experience driven by its sizeable battery.
