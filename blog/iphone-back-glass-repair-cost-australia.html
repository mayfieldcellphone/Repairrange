iPhone Back Glass Repair Cost in Australia (2026): What It Really Costs

By RepairRange · how we price · Updated 19 August 2026

Cracked iPhone back glass? Independent repairers charge $99–$279 depending on your model — but Apple-authorised work can run to $849 on some iPhones. Here's the model-by-model breakdown and when repair is actually worth it.

A cracked back is one of the most confusing iPhone repairs to price, because the answer swings wildly with the model and with who does the work. Two patterns stand out. First, the Pro Max models are the most expensive to repair — bigger glass, more antennas, and the larger battery make the job harder. Second, the iPhone 12 is in a category of its own, and it's worth understanding why before you accept a quote.

Why the iPhone 12 back glass is so expensive

The iPhone 12 was the first iPhone to bond the back glass to the aluminium chassis — there's no separate removable back panel. To replace it, a repairer has to strip out the logic board, battery and cameras, then either replace the whole chassis or do delicate glass separation work. That's why authorised repairs on the 12 run $629–$849, the most expensive back glass repair of any iPhone generation. From the iPhone 13 onwards Apple returned to a design where the back glass is a replaceable part on its own, which is why authorised pricing drops sharply from the 13 up. If you own a 12, an independent back-glass-only repair at $129–$209 is usually the sensible path.

Independent vs authorised vs DIY — what's the difference

  • Independent repairer ($99–$279): the job takes roughly 1–2 hours and typically carries a 90-day to 12-month repair warranty. This is the price to compare against when you get a shopfront quote that seems inflated.
  • Apple-authorised ($199–$849): genuine parts and Apple's own service warranty, at two to three times the independent price on most models — and on the iPhone 12, chassis-level pricing.
  • DIY kit ($45–$139): the cheapest parts, but back glass is genuinely one of the harder DIY iPhone repairs: it needs heat, careful adhesive work, and the risk of cracking the display or damaging the cameras is real. On the iPhone 17 Pro Max, RepairRange's own data rates DIY back glass as not recommended. If you're keen on DIY, see our can you DIY a phone repair guide for what's realistic.

One thing to know before any route: under the Australian Consumer Law, an independent repair does not void your consumer guarantees — see your phone repair rights in Australia.

Is it worth repairing the back glass?

Back glass is mostly cosmetic, so the maths is different from a screen repair. On a phone under about two years old that's still worth $500+, a $99–$279 repair is almost always worth it. On a four-to-five-year-old model, compare the repair against the phone's current second-hand value before committing — see our repair vs replace economic index for the framework, or run your exact model through the Repair Calculator for a precise estimate. If the crack is small, the camera works and the glass isn't lifting, a case is a legitimate short-term answer while you decide.

What about AppleCare+?

With AppleCare+, you pay the accidental-damage excess rather than the full repair cost, which changes the maths completely on Pro Max models. But whether AppleCare+ is actually worth it depends on your device's age and your history of drops — we've done the full comparison in phone insurance vs self-insuring.

FAQ

Common questions

Does a cracked iPhone back glass affect the camera?

Usually not — the camera lenses sit behind the glass, so a crack rarely affects photos unless the glass has shattered right around the lens. It's mostly cosmetic damage, but sharp cracks can snag pockets and the crack weakens the phone's water resistance, so it's worth protecting the area with a case while you decide.

Is iPhone back glass covered by warranty?

No — a crack is physical damage, which standard manufacturer warranties don't cover. The exception is if the glass cracked on its own (a hairline crack with no impact point): that can be a manufacturing defect claimable under Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees. An independent repair never voids those guarantees either.

Can I just put a case on my iPhone instead of repairing the back glass?

Yes, if the camera works and the crack isn't lifting or sharp. Back glass is largely cosmetic — the two real risks of ignoring it are glass splinters from a spreading crack and water ingress through the break. If the crack is spreading or you use your phone near water, repair becomes the cheaper long-term option.

Why is my iPhone back glass repair quote $600 or more?

On an iPhone 12 (and older models), the back glass is bonded to the aluminium chassis, so authorised repairers quote whole-chassis pricing that can run $629–$849. Independent repairers who do back-glass-only work can usually do the same job for $129–$209 on those models — always get an independent quote before accepting a chassis-level price.
