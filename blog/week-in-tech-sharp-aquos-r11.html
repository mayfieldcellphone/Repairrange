RR

Introducing the Sharp Aquos R11

User experience gets a significant upgrade with the launch of the Sharp Aquos R11. This flagship smartphone boasts a stunning 38.5MP telephoto camera that provides an impressive 2.8x optical zoom, making it ideal for capturing those beautiful Aussie landscapes or stunning close-ups of wildlife.

Under the hood, the Aquos R11 is powered by Qualcomm's latest Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset, ensuring top-notch performance whether you’re gaming, streaming, or multitasking. The device also features a vibrant 6.5-inch 240Hz OLED display, promising remarkable visuals and smooth interactions that any tech-savvy user will appreciate.

Long-lasting Power

With a robust 5,000mAh battery, the Aquos R11 is designed to keep up with your busy lifestyle, ensuring you stay connected throughout the day without the need to constantly search for a charger.

DJI Osmo Pocket 4P: A New Era for Content Creators

Also making waves this week is the announcement of the DJI Osmo Pocket 4P. Perfectly suited for both amateur and professional content creators, this compact stabiliser camera is bound to inspire creativity with its cutting-edge features.
