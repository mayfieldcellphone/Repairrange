Exploring One UI 9: What's Next for Samsung Devices Down Under

Published on June 21, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

Samsung's One UI 9 Testing Phase

Exciting news for Samsung enthusiasts in Australia! The tech giant has rolled out its third beta for One UI 9, primarily focusing on the Galaxy S26 series. But that's not all—Samsung is also gearing up for One UI 9 updates for a range of other devices.

The Future of Samsung in Australia

Currently, the Galaxy S26 is the only smartphone in the One UI 9 beta program. However, users can look forward to updates for the Galaxy S25 series, Galaxy A16 5G, Galaxy A17 5G, and the Galaxy A34. This means plenty of options for Australians looking to stay ahead with the latest software enhancements.

What Can Users Expect from One UI 9?

Performance Boosts: Users can anticipate improved speed and efficiency, with tweaks to boost the usability of apps and multitasking features.

Visual Overhauls: The user interface will likely offer new colour palettes and design elements that enhance the overall aesthetic experience.

Feature Enhancements: Expect added features that will promote seamless navigation and interactions across Samsung's expansive device ecosystem.

Getting Ready for the Update

For those keen to dive into the beta experience or simply prepare for the full launch of One UI 9, it’s important to keep your device updated. Regularly check for software updates under your settings menu. It’s also a great idea to back up your device—better safe than sorry!
