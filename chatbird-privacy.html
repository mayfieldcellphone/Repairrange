Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ChatBird ("ChatBird", "we", "us") is a shared customer inbox operated by Mayfield Phone Repair, 276 Maitland Rd, Mayfield NSW 2304, Australia. This policy explains what information ChatBird handles, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information we handle

Account information. The name and email address of people who sign in to ChatBird.

Customer messages. Messages, names and contact details that customers send to a connected business through channels the business owner has connected (for example Facebook Messenger, Instagram or SMS), together with the replies our users send.

Google user data. If, and only if, a business owner connects Google, ChatBird receives an access token for that owner's Google Business Profile account and then retrieves the business's account and location details and its customer reviews (reviewer display name, star rating, review text, review date and any reply). We request the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage.

2. How we use information

We use information only to provide the inbox: to show messages and reviews, to let authorised team members reply, to send alerts about new messages or reviews, and to keep the service secure and working. We do not use information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

ChatBird's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, for Google user data: we use it only to display the business's Google reviews and to post replies the user chooses to send; we do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the service, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to you; we do not use it for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based advertising; we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, to investigate abuse or security issues, or where law requires; and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure with access restricted to authorised users. Connection credentials and tokens, including Google tokens, are stored encrypted and are never displayed to other users. We keep information while the account or connection is active and delete or anonymise it within a reasonable time after it is disconnected or you ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help run ChatBird (such as hosting and the messaging platforms the owner has chosen to connect) and only so they can provide those services. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Your choices and rights

A business owner can disconnect Google at any time from ChatBird's Integrations page, and can also remove ChatBird's access from their Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing the address below. If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
