Flat Out Fuming: Galaxy Z Flip8 Skips Charging Upgrade, Again!

Published on June 06, 2026 • Written by RepairRange Editorial

G'day tech enthusiasts and fellow foldable fanatics! Here at RepairRange.io, we're always keen to spill the beans on the latest mobile phone developments, especially when they hit our Aussie shores. But today, we've got a bit of a head-scratcher – a fresh slab of news that's leaving us feeling a tad flat, much like a smartphone that's been on the charger for ages.

The buzz around Samsung's upcoming Galaxy Z Flip8 has taken a bit of a dive, and not in a good, sleek-folding way. Recent certifications from SGS have all but confirmed what many of us were hoping was just an unfounded rumour: the Z Flip8 will reportedly stick with a rather pedestrian 25W wired charging speed. That's right, folks – the same 25W we've seen since the Galaxy Z Flip4. Crikey, that's four generations without a sniff of an upgrade!

For those of us who live life in the fast lane – commuting, working, socialising, and trying to squeeze every last drop out of our devices before the next coffee run – 25W is, frankly, a bit of a snail's pace in today's mobile landscape. Imagine needing a quick power-up before heading out from the city to the 'burbs; you're not going to be thrilled waiting around for your premium foldable to crawl back to life. In a world where some flagships are pushing 80W, 100W, or even more, 25W feels like Samsung is taking the mickey.

It begs the question: why the stagnation? Is it a thermal challenge within the compact foldable design? A strategic decision to differentiate from their S-series? Or perhaps a cost-cutting measure that, frankly, doesn't sit well when you're shelling out top dollar (likely well over AUD$1,500) for a cutting-edge device. Regardless of the reason, it's a definite bummer for those hoping for a truly all-round upgraded experience with the Flip8.

Why Repair Costs Matter for Your Premium Flip

While the charging speed might be a point of contention, let's talk about something even more critical for long-term ownership of these fantastic, yet sometimes delicate, foldable devices. The Galaxy Z Flip series, with its innovative folding screen, is a marvel of engineering, but that complexity also means potential repair costs can be substantial, especially for the display and battery.

Screen repairs on foldable phones are notoriously expensive. The intricate hinge mechanism and the flexible display technology mean that even a seemingly minor drop or internal fault with the screen crease can lead to a hefty bill. Given the premium price tag of a Z Flip8, understanding potential repair costs is paramount to protecting your investment.

Similarly, while the charging speed isn't changing, the battery itself will still degrade over time with daily use and countless charging cycles. A slow charging speed might even tempt some users to keep their phone plugged in longer, which can also impact battery longevity. Eventually, you'll want to replace that battery to keep your device performing optimally.
