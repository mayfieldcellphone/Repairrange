What a Pre-Repair Inspection Should Actually Cover (Before You Hand Over Your Phone)

Quick Benchmark

What to Check Before Handing Your Phone Over

A thorough pre-repair check takes three minutes and protects both you and the technician. Before leaving the counter, test both cameras, earpiece and loud speaker, Face ID or fingerprint recognition, wireless charging, and cellular reception. Ensure the shop logs existing frame dents and signs off on working sub-components in writing before taking possession.

The moment you hand your damaged smartphone across a service counter, an unwritten legal and technical transfer occurs. If your front microphone was already dead from liquid seepage two months ago, but neither you nor the shop noticed before replacing the screen, who pays when you discover the fault after pick-up?

At independent workshops across Australia, dispute logs reveal that over half of customer conflicts do not stem from the repair itself, but from pre-existing secondary failures that went undocumented at intake. A cracked glass panel absorbs impact energy, but that same kinetic shockwave frequently compromises proximity sensors, optical image stabilisation (OIS) coils, or face-recognition projectors. Here is the exact intake protocol professional bench technicians use, and how you can run it yourself to protect your device, your data, and your wallet.

1. The 12-Point Pre-Intake Counter Inspection

If a technician takes your phone, scribbles your name on an adhesive post-it note, and quotes you an arrival time without testing basic hardware functions in front of you, ask them to pause. Legitimate workshops follow an intake checklist. If you are dropping a device off in Newcastle, Sydney, or Melbourne, verify these twelve items together:

Component How to Test in 10 Seconds Why It Fails During Drops
TrueDepth / Face ID Open Camera app > Portrait Mode (front camera) The dot projector and flood illuminator are suspended on fragile silicon ribbons prone to impact tears.
Proximity Sensor Make a test call and place your palm over the earpiece Mounted directly behind the upper screen edge; easily misaligned by hairline glass fracture patterns.
Rear Camera OIS Open Camera at 1x zoom and listen for rattling or humming Electromagnetic suspension springs snap upon concrete contact, causing permanent blurry focus.
Microphone Array Record a 5-second voice memo and a front selfie video Smartphones use 3 to 4 distinct mics; bottom for calls, top for speakerphone, rear for video recordings.
Ear Speaker & Loudspeaker Play a ringtone, then toggle to regular phone ear speaker Fine acoustic mesh often compacts with pocket lint or tears under direct surface impact.
NFC & Wireless Coil Hold near payment terminal or tap onto wireless charging mat The rear glass adhesive secures the charging coil; structural flexing can sever delicate copper traces.

If any feature is non-functional before disassembly, having it written onto the intake receipt establishes that the repair technician did not break it during opening. Conversely, if all twelve items pass inspection on the counter, the technician is strictly accountable for returning the phone with those same twelve features working perfectly.

2. Liquid Contact Indicators (LCI): Check Them Together

Every modern Apple, Samsung, and Google handset contains Liquid Contact Indicators (LCIs) placed inside the SIM card tray slot, charging port, and internal shield plates. Normally pure white or metallic silver, these paper patches turn bright crimson pink upon contact with moisture.

Unscrupulous operators have occasionally blamed subsequent board failures on "liquid damage" after a botch-job assembly. Before relinquishing the phone, shine a penlight into the SIM tray recess. If the white indicator is visible and untouched, verify that the intake sheet explicitly marks "Liquid Damage: None / White LCI". If moisture is present, you need to know immediately, as standard screen warranties will not apply to water-damaged motherboards.

3. Securing Your Personal Data Without Giving Up Passcodes

One of the most common anxieties Australian phone owners express is handing over their lock screen passcode. Why does a technician need full access to your device to fit a piece of glass?

While technicians do need to test display touch digitisation, multitouch responsiveness, and audio clarity, they do not need your private photo library or banking applications. You have three practical options:

  • Samsung Maintenance Mode: If you use a modern Galaxy device, go to Settings > Battery and Device Care > Maintenance Mode. This boots a clean temporary profile that hides all accounts, photos, and messages while leaving testing tools fully functional.
  • Android Diagnostic Test Menus: Dialing *#0*# on Samsung devices launches a hardware test harness directly from the emergency dialler without entering the device password.
  • Apple Guided Access or Full iCloud Backup: For iPhone users, complete a fresh encrypted backup via Finder or iCloud before visiting the shop. If you choose not to share your PIN, agree to test the handset in person at the counter before completing final payment.

4. Frame Squaring and Structural Alignment

A phone screen is an unyielding sheet of tempered aluminosilicate glass glued to a display module. A phone chassis, however, is malleable aluminium or titanium. When a phone hits tarmac on a corner, the metal bezel compresses or twists inward.

If a repairer glues a brand new, flat display into a pinched frame without filing down the corner burrs or squaring the chassis on a jig, that screen will sit under torsion. Even a gentle bump in your back pocket three days later will cause spontaneous glass fracture. Ensure the intake note specifies whether corner smoothing or frame realignment is required as part of the job.

5. Upfront Quotes vs Diagnostic Fees: What ACL Protects

Under Australian Consumer Law (ACL), businesses cannot quote a "starting price" and later hit you with compulsory supplemental fees without your prior agreement. Before approving work, confirm whether the price quoted includes:

  • Parts, skilled bench labour, and GST
  • Pre-installed screen adhesive seals (re-waterproofing gasket)
  • A written warranty duration (look for minimum 6 to 12 months on parts)
