Methodology

How we get to a number.

Repair prices vary widely by region, part quality, and shop overhead. Rather than publish a single number that's wrong everywhere, we publish ranges that reflect what real shops charge.

Every model price is sourced from a minimum of nine quotes across surveyed independent shops and authorised service centres. We re-survey quarterly.

01

We quote, not parrot

Prices come from real shop quotes, not press releases or affiliate referral schemes.

02

DIY costs are real costs

The "DIY" price includes the part, the right tools, and the realistic ~25% chance you'll need a second attempt.

03

Independent of any one shop

No shop pays us to be listed. Lead-gen forms route to verified local techs based on proximity.

04

Re-priced quarterly

Phone parts get cheaper as they age. Every model is re-surveyed each quarter.
